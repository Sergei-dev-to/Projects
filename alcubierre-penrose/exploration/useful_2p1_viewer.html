Reading the geometry

Orange: boundary of where the rider can send signals. Blue: boundary of what can send signals to the rider. The overlap is the region allowing communication both ways.

Small wire cones are local tangent light cones, not finite geodesic segments. Dashed-looking rays in the static figure are integrated null geodesics; the colored horizon curves follow null generators.

The cap is reached as original time t tends to infinity. The regular coordinates continue through it at finite traveler proper time. No finite-time edge in the main view is a spacetime boundary.
