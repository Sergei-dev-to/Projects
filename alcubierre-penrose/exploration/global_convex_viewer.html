What is checked

Global injectivity follows from strict convexity. The metric is regular and Lorentzian on every finite-cut scri face and every open cap crossing. Both boundary normals are null.

This is a conformal representation with a singular boundary, not a flat Minkowski diagram, a minimal Penrose completion, or a diagram of a maximal physical extension.
