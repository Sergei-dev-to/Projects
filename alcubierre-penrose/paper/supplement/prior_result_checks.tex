\section*{Checks of the local background results}

The following calculations reproduce the local spherical tidal behavior
already encoded in the transverse dynamics of
Ref.~\cite[Secs.~3.1--3.3]{BarceloEtAl2022}.  They fix curvature conventions
and verify the exact stationary-generator formula; they are not used as
an independent novelty claim.  The manuscript instead proves global
four-dimensional inextendibility and the cap criterion.

\subsection*{Spherical stationary-generator check}
\label{background:sec:obstruction}

The reduced metric omits the curvature measured by transverse separations
between freely falling trajectories.  For a nondegenerate radial wall
\(r=R_F\), put
\(\kappa=-v_r(R_F)>0\) and
\(\chi=\partial_t\).  Although \(\chi\) is null on the wall, the
four-dimensional wall worldvolume is generally timelike:
\begin{equation}
  g^{ab}\partial_a(v^2-1)\partial_b(v^2-1)=4(v_y^2+v_z^2).
  \label{background:eq:wall-normal-norm}
\end{equation}
On \(v=-1\),
\begin{equation}
  \nabla_{\partial_t}\partial_t
    =v_x\partial_t+v_y\partial_y+v_z\partial_z .
  \label{background:eq:not-whole-sphere}
\end{equation}
Thus only the two axial stationary null orbits are geodesic up to
reparametrization.  At the future front,
\begin{equation}
  \nabla_{\partial_t}\partial_t=-\kappa\partial_t .
  \label{background:eq:nonaffine}
\end{equation}
Here \(\kappa\) measures the exponential rescaling between stationary time
and affine parametrization on this orbit.  It is a local inaffinity and
peeling scale \cite{CroppLiberatiVisser2013}.  On a genuine Killing-horizon
patch, \(-\kappa\) is the signed surface gravity for \(\chi=\partial_t\).
An affine tangent is \(K=q\chi\), with
\(q=[\kappa(\lambda_*-\lambda)]^{-1}\).  The transverse vectors
\(Y=\partial_y\) and \(Z=\partial_z\) are parallel along the orbit, and
\(H_{AB}:=v_{AB}\), with \(A,B\in\{y,z\}\), satisfies
\begin{equation}
  H_{AB}=-\frac{\kappa}{R_F}\delta_{AB}.
  \label{background:eq:radial-hessian}
\end{equation}
A direct curvature calculation gives
\begin{equation}
  R(K,Y,K,Y)=R(K,Z,K,Z)
  =-\frac{1}{\kappa R_F(\lambda_*-\lambda)^2}.
  \label{background:eq:main-blowup}
\end{equation}
The isometry \((t,x,y,z)\mapsto(-t,-x,y,z)\) gives the past-rear result.
These are parallel-propagated curvature singularities
\cite{EllisSchmidt1977}.  Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-frames} excludes a \(C^2\)
endpoint.  Scalar curvature measures, studied for these geometries in
Refs.~\cite{MattinglyEtAl2021,Rodal2023}, remain finite and miss this
obstruction.  The Killing-field calculation below gives the curvature
identities and derivation.

Flattening the front removes this transverse tidal term at the chosen
generator.  A regular extension requires a whole planar patch with constant
surface gravity, as Theorem~\ref{thm:extension-rigidity} shows.

\subsection*{Killing-field curvature check}
\label{background:app:local-curvature}

Write \(\Sigma=\{v=-1\}\).  Its stationary-generator locus is
\begin{equation}
  \mathcal G=\{p\in\Sigma:v_y(p)=v_z(p)=0\}.
  \label{background:eq:generator-locus}
\end{equation}
For any Killing field \(\chi\), the Killing equation and Ricci identity give
\begin{equation}
  R(\chi,X,\chi,Y)
   =g(\nabla_X\chi,\nabla_Y\chi)
    -\frac12(\nabla^2\chi^2)(X,Y).
  \label{background:eq:killing-tidal-identity}
\end{equation}
With
\((\nabla_c\nabla_d-\nabla_d\nabla_c)V^a=R^a{}_{bcd}V^b\), this follows from
\(\nabla_a\nabla_b\chi_c=R_{cbad}\chi^d\) by differentiating \(\chi^2\).
For the scalar shift, take \(\chi=\partial_t\) and introduce the stationary
orthonormal frame
\begin{equation}
  e_0=\partial_t+v\partial_x,\qquad e_1=\partial_x,
  \qquad e_A=\partial_A.
  \label{background:eq:stationary-frame}
\end{equation}
On \(\Sigma\), Eq.~\eqref{background:eq:not-whole-sphere} gives the stationary acceleration.
Thus \(\mathcal G\) is precisely the locus of null pregeodesic stationary
orbits, and \(e_0+e_1=\chi\) there.  At \(p\in\mathcal G\), the transverse,
or screen, components of the Killing identity reduce to
\begin{equation}
  H_{AB}(p):=v_{AB}(p),
  \qquad
  R_{tAtB}=H_{AB},
  \qquad R_{KAKB}=H_{AB}q^2
  \quad\text{for }K=q\partial_t .
  \label{background:eq:screen-general}
\end{equation}

Let the null orbit through \(p\) be parametrized by Killing time and suppose
\(\nabla_\chi\chi=\alpha\chi\), with \(\alpha\ne0\).  At one end of the orbit,
its affine tangent \(K=q\chi\) obeys
\(|q|=[|\alpha|\,|\lambda-\lambda_*|]^{-1}\).  Thus a nonzero transverse tidal
form produces a parallel-propagated curvature singularity proportional to
\(|\lambda-\lambda_*|^{-2}\).  At \(p\in\mathcal G\), \(v_A=0\) also gives
\(\nabla_\chi e_A=0\), so the coordinate screen vectors themselves are parallel.
Lemma~\ref{lem:endpoint-frames} therefore excludes a \(C^2\) endpoint.
