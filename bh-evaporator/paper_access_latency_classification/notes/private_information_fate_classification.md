# Private-Information Fate Classification

Date: 2026-06-16

Purpose: state the largest current result target in one place: a constrained-access classification of what can happen to private quantum information behind a public record layer.

## Classification Question

Given:

- a source Hilbert space or code algebra,
- a public/passive record channel,
- an enlarged access channel or decoder class,
- and possibly internal dynamics,

what is the fate of information that is not in the redundantly recoverable public center?

The target is not another analogy between horizons and measurement cuts. The target is a mechanism classification:

```text
relative to a specified access channel, private information first falls
into a compartment; recovery mechanisms then say how, or whether, it
becomes recoverable when access is enlarged.
```

## Static Compartments

These are mutually exclusive relative to the current passive record algebra.

### 1. Public Center

Information becomes part of the redundant classical center. Many disjoint fragments recover the same sector label.

Mathematical signature:

```text
redundant recovery from disjoint fragments
=> approximately/exactly commutative public algebra.
```

Status:

- Exact version is in `main.tex` as the cut theorem and public-center corollary.
- Approximate/stability version is imported from no-broadcasting, Quantum Darwinism, and SBS literature.

### 2. Recorded But Deep Block

Information couples to the record algebra, but small fragments or shallow decoders cannot recover it. It is not protected by the commutant, but it is not public either.

Mathematical signature:

```text
O not in A_rec'
but recovery requires large fragments / many records / deep decoding.
```

Status:

- Conceptually present.
- Needs sharper quantitative diagnostics.
- The effective irreducibility rate `lambda` measures one side of this: how fast protected components collapse into the recorded algebra.
- A separate decodability estimate is needed to say when recorded information is actually recoverable.

### 3. Protected Commutant

Information lies in the commutant/noiseless subsystem of the current record-generating algebra. Passive records cannot reveal it at all until the access algebra changes.

Mathematical signature:

```text
O in A_rec'
```

or blockwise:

```text
H = direct_sum_alpha H_alpha^L tensor H_alpha^R,
A_rec = direct_sum_alpha B(H_alpha^L) tensor I_alpha^R.
```

Status:

- Proven in `main.tex` as the protected-complement commutant theorem.
- The collective-charge pointer is the model witness.

## Recovery Mechanisms

These are not additional compartments. They describe what happens when the access channel is enlarged, records accumulate, or internal dynamics acts.

### A. Slow Routed

Information is recoverable only after it physically reaches the access region through finite-velocity dynamics.

Mathematical signature:

```text
t_rec >= L/v - logarithmic corrections.
```

For a `d`-dimensional reservoir with entropy `S` and worst-case bulk deposits:

```text
t_rec = Omega(S^{1/d})
```

Status:

- Proven in `main.tex` as the reduced-record Lieb-Robinson latency theorem.
- Ordinary local reservoirs and saturated slow routers fall here.

### B. Fast Routed / Scrambled

Information becomes recoverable because internal dynamics brings arbitrary private deposits to the access/emission channel in sub-power-law time.

Mathematical signature:

```text
t_routing = polylog(S)
```

or, in horizon language,

```text
t_scr ~ beta log S
```

with the appropriate Page/Hayden-Preskill side-information condition for actual recovery.

Status:

- Classified by the mechanism corollary.
- Not derived from source-local finite-velocity dynamics.
- The missing model-side task is to derive the effective irreducibility/de-protection rate in a concrete deterministic fast-mixing record model.

### C. Dressed / Nonlocal Accessible

Information is recoverable because the allowed access algebra is nonlocal relative to the naive factorized source variables.

Mathematical signature:

```text
A_allowed is not generated by source-local emission near X.
```

Examples:

- dense nonlocal encoder,
- boundary reconstruction,
- gauge/gravitational dressing,
- side information that is nonlocal relative to the source factorization.

Status:

- Classified by the mechanism corollary and frozen-dynamics diagnostic.
- Recovery that survives frozen source-local routing belongs here, unless the side information already trivially contains the diary.

## No-Recovery Verdict

### Effectively Forgotten Relative to the Channel

Information is not recoverable by the specified allowed channel. This is a statement relative to the access model, not necessarily a statement of global destruction.

Mathematical signature:

```text
N_allowed is constant or nearly constant on the diary,
```

or no allowed decoder achieves order-one entanglement fidelity.

Status:

- Lemma A / constant-channel bound supplies the local obstruction.
- In unitary global models this is effective forgetting, not fundamental erasure.
- This verdict can apply to recorded-but-deep or protected information if the allowed recovery resources never enlarge enough to reveal it.

## Existing Theorem Coverage

Current `main.tex` already covers the following:

```text
publicized:
    cut theorem / public-center corollary

protected:
    commutant theorem / irreducibility criterion

slow routed:
    reduced-record LR latency theorem

fast routed or dressed:
    mechanism classification corollary

branch diagnostic:
    frozen-dynamics diagnostic

saturation is insufficient:
    saturated slow router theorem

anonymity is insufficient:
    collective-charge / anonymous-saturation theorem
```

So the classification is not aspirational from scratch. It is mostly assembled.

## Missing Gates

### Gate 1: De-protection Rate

Need a concrete model where

```text
|| E_n(O_private) ||_2 <= C exp(-lambda n)
```

can be proved or falsified.

This tells when protected commutant information stops being protected as records accumulate.

Candidate assumptions:

- random record generators,
- ETH-like jump operators,
- unitary designs,
- spectral gap of the algebra-growth / twirling channel,
- fast-scrambling circuit model.

First solvable model now in `main.tex`:

```text
q-qubit private block;
n independent uniformly random nonidentity Pauli record generators;
A_n = algebra generated by sampled Paulis.
```

For a fixed traceless private operator `O`,

```text
E ||E_n(O)||_2^2 = p_q^n,
p_q = (D^2/2 - 1)/(D^2 - 1) < 1/2.
```

For worst-case exact protection,

```text
Pr[A_n' != C I] <= (D^2-1) p_q^n <= D^2 2^{-n}.
```

Thus fixed operators de-protect exponentially per independent record generator, while worst-case exact protected subsystems collapse after

```text
n >= 2q + log_2(1/delta) + O(1)
```

with probability at least `1-delta`.

This is useful but also diagnostic: exact worst-case protection is a spanning/commutant-collapse question, while fixed-deposit de-protection is an exponential-weight question.

For a horizon-sized public block with `D ~ exp(S)`, worst-case exact commutant collapse at `O(log D)` record generators is `O(S)`, linear in entropy. That is stronger than Hayden-Preskill recovery needs. HP asks whether a specific deposited `k`-qubit diary becomes decodable; the Pauli baseline gives fixed-diary de-protection in

```text
O(k + log(1/epsilon) + log(1/delta))
```

independent record units by a union bound over the diary Pauli basis.

The iid Pauli model is therefore a best-case baseline, not the physical scrambling theorem. In a dynamical model the generators are correlated Heisenberg iterates, schematically

```text
P_i = U^{-i} K U^i.
```

The physical question is whether chaotic/ETH dynamics makes these iterates effectively independent. The usual `log S` scrambling scale is a time cost for manufacturing independent record units, not the number of independent records required once they are available.

### Gate 2: Decodability After De-protection

De-protection is not recovery. Once information leaves the commutant, one still needs a decoder or complementary-channel estimate.

Need to state clearly:

```text
commutant collapse:
    information is no longer exactly protected

recovery:
    information can be reconstructed from allowed records
```

Page/Hayden-Preskill, decoupling, Petz recovery, or channel-capacity conditions belong here.

Baseline result now in `main.tex`:

```text
post-Page random coding;
diary D entangled with reference R_D;
old block B maximally entangled with early radiation E;
random U: DB -> RC.
```

Standard decoupling gives

```text
E_U || rho_{R_D C} - rho_{R_D} tensor rho_C ||_1
    <= O(d_D/d_R).
```

Therefore, after

```text
log_2 d_R >= k + 2 log_2(1/epsilon) + log_2(1/delta) + O(1)
```

new radiation qubits, with probability at least `1-delta` there is a decoder from `R E` to the diary with entanglement fidelity `1 - O(epsilon)`.

This closes the random baseline:

```text
independent randomization
=> fixed-diary de-protection
=> fixed-diary recovery.
```

The remaining physical gate is not the random baseline. It is replacing independent randomization by correlated deterministic dynamics:

```text
P_i = U^{-i} K U^i,
```

and proving that after scrambling-time separation the effective record units satisfy the de-protection and decoupling hypotheses well enough.

### Gate 3: Approximate Public Stability

The exact public-center theorem is clean but idealized. A reviewable version should eventually cite or import a stability statement:

```text
approximate redundant recovery
=> approximately commuting public algebra / approximate SBS structure.
```

This is probably literature import, not a new theorem target for the current draft.

## Result Shape

The eventual theorem statement should look like:

> For a constrained-access interface, private information decomposes relative to the current access algebra into public-center data, recorded-but-deep data, and protected commutant data. Under source-local finite-velocity dynamics, recovery of arbitrary remote private deposits is power-law delayed. Sub-power-law location-uniform recovery therefore requires either fast routing/scrambling that collapses the protected component and makes the record channel decodable, or a nonlocal/dressed access algebra. Saturation, anonymity, and unitary record formation alone do not imply either mechanism.

This is the classification result. The iid-Pauli model supplies a baseline fixed-deposit `lambda`, and random decoupling supplies the corresponding baseline recovery theorem. The deterministic problem splits into two parts:

```text
observability:
    does alg{U^{-i} K U^i} see the diary algebra at all?

export/decoupling:
    does the emitted record system actually recover the diary?
```

The exact observability layer is now in `main.tex`: protected information under a sampled Heisenberg orbit is the commutant of its observability algebra; generic full algebra generation is open once one full example exists; symmetries, selection rules, fragmentation, collective monitoring, and small operator Krylov spaces are exact obstruction classes.

The approximate layer is also isolated. For one-step sampled record algebras `A_i`, let `Pi_i` be the Hilbert-Schmidt conditional expectation onto `A_i'` and define

```text
T_{1:n} = Pi_n ... Pi_1.
```

An observability gap `gamma` on the diary algebra means

```text
||T_{1:n}(O_D)||_2 <= C exp(-gamma n) ||O_D||_2.
```

Since the exact protected algebra is the intersection of the one-step commutants, this implies

```text
||E_n(O_D)||_2 <= C exp(-gamma n) ||O_D||_2.
```

So the gap is now the precise bridge from deterministic observability to fixed-diary de-protection.

The benchmark proposition now gives three checks:

```text
iid Pauli records:
    average squared-norm gap >= log 2 + O(D^-2)

common non-scalar commutant:
    gamma = 0

conditional per-step contraction:
    E[||Pi_i O||_2^2 | past] <= (1-eta)||O||_2^2
    => E||T_{1:n}O||_2^2 <= exp(-eta n)||O||_2^2.
```

The third line is the current bridge to deterministic dynamics. It does not assume iid records; it asks for a contraction after conditioning on the past.

## External-Review Adjustment

The first outside-style review confirms the main classification stack but
sharpens the next target:

```text
source-local finite-velocity emission
=> information-theoretic latency obstruction
=> fast recovery requires fast routing/scrambling or nonlocal/dressed access
```

This is the strongest current result. The deterministic positive side is
still the open gate. The iid Pauli and Haar/decoupling baselines are
calibration models, not the physical theorem.

The deterministic gap should now be attacked with tools that already
know about correlated Heisenberg dynamics:

```text
operator spreading / OTOCs:
    growth of [U^{-i} K U^i, O_D]

unitary designs / frame potentials:
    when deterministic dynamics manufactures effectively independent
    record generators

free probability / asymptotic freeness:
    contraction between diary algebra and rotated record subalgebras

quantum-control observability:
    dynamical Lie algebra and observability-Gramian analogues
```

The product of one-step commutant projections remains a sufficient
certificate. It should not be treated as the only possible approximate
observability measure. For deterministic sequences it is an
alternating-projections/Friedrichs-angle object; a weak bound there may
mean the certificate is conservative rather than that de-protection
fails.

## Current Next Move

The next result target has shifted after the access/export synthesis.
The deterministic observability problem remains important, but the
nearest positive theorem is the theorem-backed expander mixer:

```text
bounded-degree/log-diameter access geometry
+ explicit TPE/design or random-circuit decoupling primitive
+ HP export budget
=> logarithmic or polylogarithmic location-uniform private recovery.
```

This proves the fast-routing branch is nonempty under explicit
information-theoretic mixing assumptions. It is the right first positive
existence theorem because it cleanly separates:

```text
access geometry:
    expander/log-diameter routing

export:
    theorem-backed decoupling primitive
```

The next strengthening after that is the deterministic replacement
problem:

```text
fixed record coupling K;
deterministic scrambling dynamics U, ideally on an expander graph;
P_i = U^{-i} K U^i;
A_n = algebra generated by the pulled-back record operators;
prove an observability/de-protection rate on the diary algebra;
then separately prove decoupling/export for the emitted records.
```

If the deterministic orbit reproduces the theorem-backed mixer behavior,
the classification gains a physical fast-routing mechanism. If it fails,
the classification becomes sharper: fast recovery requires stronger
mixing/export assumptions than generic operator growth.
