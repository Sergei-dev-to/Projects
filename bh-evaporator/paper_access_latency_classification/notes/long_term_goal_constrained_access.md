# Long-Term Goal: Constrained Access and Emergence

Date: 2026-06-16

Purpose: state the program-level destination behind the access-latency and private-information classification work. This is not a theorem note. It is the compass for deciding which technical steps matter.

## Goal

Understand whether classical reality, horizons, and perhaps effective geometry can be described as consequences of constrained access to quantum information.

Operational form:

```text
large quantum substrate
    + restricted observers / records / controls
    -> public algebra
    -> recorded-but-deep block
    -> private/protected complement
    -> recovery and latency laws
    -> effective classical or horizon-like structure above the cut
```

The long-term target is not merely to compare black holes with measurement cuts. It is to understand what kinds of effective worlds are induced by restricted access channels.

## Core Hypothesis

The world seen by an observer is not the full substrate. It is the effective algebra exposed by that observer's access channel.

```text
substrate Hilbert space
    -> constrained access channel
    -> public/deep/private decomposition
```

In this language:

- classical facts are the public center;
- hard but present quantum information is the recorded-but-deep block;
- exactly invisible passive information is the commutant/noiseless subsystem;
- horizons are extreme constrained-access interfaces;
- substrate independence is access equivalence: different microscopic substrates can expose the same effective accessible channel.

## Why Horizons Matter

Black holes are the stress test because the public exterior channel is tiny while the private complement is enormous:

```text
public:
    mass, charge, angular momentum, temperature, coarse radiation records

private:
    microstate and diary information

recovery:
    Page/Hayden-Preskill, fast scrambling, or dressed/nonlocal access
```

The information puzzle becomes the distinction between:

```text
effective forgetting:
    the public channel cannot distinguish the information

recoverable hiding:
    the public channel cannot distinguish it, but a larger quantum channel can
```

Horizon physics forces those two notions apart.

## Why Measurement Cuts Matter

The Heisenberg cut is the ordinary constrained-access interface:

```text
public:
    stable redundant measurement records

private:
    coherence, phases, lab/friend degrees, non-public correlations

moving the cut:
    quantum eraser, Wigner operation, enlarged control
```

The cut and the horizon need not be identical. The useful question is whether they share the same public/deep/private access skeleton, with different dynamics and different costs for changing access.

## Where Geometry Might Enter

The speculative ceiling is that effective locality or geometry may be reconstructed from access costs rather than assumed as microscopic input.

Possible dictionary:

```text
near:
    cheap recovery / direct coupling / low latency

far:
    recovery requires routing / larger fragments / higher latency

behind a horizon:
    public channel sees coarse data; private recovery needs scrambling or dressed access
```

This is not yet a result. The current work only supports the algebraic and latency side. Geometry above the cut would require additional consistency conditions: stable low-dimensional locality, compatible overlapping observers, Lorentz/Newtonian limits where appropriate, and correct semiclassical horizon behavior.

## Current Foothold

The present access-latency draft gives the first rigorous foothold:

```text
public center:
    exact redundant records imply commutative classical data

protected private complement:
    passive-record privacy is the commutant/noiseless subsystem

latency:
    finite-velocity source-local access gives power-law delay

mechanism classification:
    fast private recovery requires fast routing/scrambling or nonlocal/dressed access

random baseline:
    iid Pauli growth de-protects a fixed diary quickly
    random coding makes the diary recoverable after O(k + log 1/epsilon) records
```

So the current result is not the emergence of geometry. It is a constrained-access fate classification for private information.

## Main Missing Gate

The random baseline must be replaced by physical dynamics:

```text
fresh independent random generators
    -> correlated Heisenberg orbit P_i = U^{-i} K U^i
```

The next major theorem would show that chaotic/ETH dynamics produces sufficiently independent record pullbacks after a scrambling-time cost, so that fixed-diary de-protection and decodability follow in a physical model.

If this works, the program has a real bridge:

```text
access algebra
    -> private complement
    -> scrambling/de-protection
    -> recovery
    -> horizon-like constrained access
```

If it fails, the classification still improves: fast recovery requires stronger structure than generic chaotic record growth.

## What Not To Claim Yet

Do not claim that gravity has been derived.

Do not claim that geometry emerges from the access algebra alone.

Do not claim that horizons are literally Wigner-friend cuts.

Do not claim that RG-as-channel is new.

The current defensible claim is:

> Constrained access gives a rigorous public/deep/private decomposition and a recovery-latency classification for private quantum information. Horizons and measurement cuts can be compared through this structure, and geometry may eventually be approached as an effective structure above the access cut.
