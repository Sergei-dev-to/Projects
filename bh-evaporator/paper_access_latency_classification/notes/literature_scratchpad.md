## Wigner Friend and Black Holes

- Hausmann and Renner, "The firewall paradox is Wigner's friend paradox," arXiv:2504.03835.
  - Direct prior art for the firewall/Wigner-friend analogy.
  - Useful for perspective-combination, no single observer having all outcomes, and Hayden-Preskill-like reversal as Wigner-friend reversal.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.03835

- Walleghem, "Wigner's friend's black hole adventure," arXiv:2507.05369.
  - Direct prior art for combining Wigner-friend protocols with black-hole decoding/firewall/no-cloning paradoxes.
  - Useful as caution that "black holes are Wigner friend" is not a new thesis.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.05369

- Adlam, "What do black holes teach us about Wigner's Friend?"
  - Lower-priority adjacent foundations/philosophy source.
  - Search result suggested relationality/retrocausality framing around black-hole paradoxes and Wigner-friend setups.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.17839

## Wigner Friend, Memory, and Quantum Erasure

- Elouard et al., "Quantum erasing the memory of Wigner's friend," arXiv:2009.09905.
  - Important for the fixed-cut versus moving-cut distinction.
  - Full Wigner control can erase the friend's memory and restore interference; leaving memory degrees untouched changes the operational context.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.09905

- Relano, "Decoherence framework for Wigner's friend experiments," arXiv:1908.09737.
  - Useful for the point that external interference operations can modify memory records.
  - Supports treating records/access as physical, not merely epistemic.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1908.09737

  - Probably lower priority unless the private-record discussion needs a memory-specific citation.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.15497

## Observer and Access Algebras in Gravity

- Witten, "Algebras, Regions, and Observers," arXiv:2303.02837.
  - Important gravity-side support for observer-access algebras.
  - Region algebras are not the clean primitive in gravity; the operational algebra accessible to an observer/worldline is central.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2303.02837

## Hayden-Preskill and Recovery Experiments/Models

- "Hayden-Preskill recovery in chaotic and integrable unitary circuit dynamics," Quantum 2024.

  - Useful baseline for recovery latency and local/integrable versus chaotic comparison.
  - Link: https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2024-08-08-1434/

- Yoshida and Kitaev, "Efficient decoding for the Hayden-Preskill protocol."
  - Standard decoding technology; found through related HP search results.
  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03363

- Cheng et al., "Realizing the Hayden-Preskill protocol with coupled Dicke models."
  - Physical model for HP recovery in coupled Dicke systems.

  - Link: https://link.aps.org/doi/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.2.043024

  - HP variant with projective measurement, local operations on early radiation.

  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.14207

  - Link: https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.13493

## Current Read on the Gap

The recent search supports three conclusions:

1. The Wigner-friend / black-hole analogy is prior art.
2. Public objectivity and redundant records are heavily studied under Quantum Darwinism.
3. The less occupied target is the private-complement diagnostic: after public records saturate, what resources reveal information that remains inaccessible to the public channel?

That target should be developed through access algebras, commutants/noiseless subsystems, recovery latency, and frozen-dynamics diagnostics rather than through another Wigner-friend paradox protocol.
