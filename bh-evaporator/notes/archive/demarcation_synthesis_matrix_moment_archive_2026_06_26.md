## The Moment Formulation

Let `a` label a background-tethered D0 site and `m` label a resolvable emitted
record: cluster size, momentum bin, and energy bin.

```text
V_{a,m}: H_frac(N,E)
  -> H_frac(N-q,E-omega_m) tensor R_m.
```

The emitted-record operator seen at fixed resolution is the collective sum

```text
V_m = sum_a V_{a,m},
```

because the outgoing record labels the emitted cluster, momentum bin, and
energy bin, not the background site that produced it.

The inclusive first-moment object is

```text
Gamma_m = V_m^\dagger V_m.
```

With the collective sum expanded,

```text
Gamma_m =
  sum_a V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{a,m}
  + sum_{a != b} V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{b,m}.
```

The first-moment condition is:

```text
<Gamma_m>_microcanonical
  reproduces the BFK/Hawking inclusive rate.
```

BFK's rate calculation corresponds to the case where the cross-source terms
vanish after the relevant microcanonical or phase average:

```text
<V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{b,m}>_microcanonical ~ 0,  a != b,

<Gamma_m>_microcanonical
  ~ sum_a <V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{a,m}>_microcanonical.
```

This is a first-moment incoherence condition.

The second-moment condition is an ETH-like statement for microcanonical bases:

```text
E[(V_m)_{beta r, alpha}
  overline{(V_n)_{beta' r', alpha'}}]

  ~ delta_mn delta_rr' delta_alphaalpha' delta_betabeta'
    F_m(E,omega) / dim H_frac(N,E),
```

up to smooth energy dependence and symmetry constraints.

The same test can also be applied before the collective sum, to the local
pieces `V_{a,m}`. The local test asks whether individual attachment sites have
ETH-like matrix elements. The collective test asks whether the physically
resolved outgoing record preserves enough of that randomness after summing over
sites. The second question is the one directly relevant to information export.

If the collective second moment holds after scrambling, standard decoupling
gives generic information export. If the collective operator has a low-rank
second moment or only sees coarse collective labels, the same substrate can
reproduce Hawking thermodynamics while leaving generic export unproven.

BFK-style incoherence is only the first step. The statement that
cross-source terms vanish in the inclusive rate,

```text
<V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{b,m}>_microcanonical ~ 0, a != b,
```

does not by itself prove high participation of the channel-doorway Gram
matrix. It fixes the trace-scale rate. The export question asks whether the
full family of detachment doorways spans many independent exit-channel
directions after daughter states are included.

## First-Moment Non-Identifiability Lemma

The first moment cannot decide information export.

Let the input shell factor as `H_i = H_D tensor H_C`, where `D` is a diary
subsystem and `C` is the remaining black-hole state. Let the emission record
have bins `m` with fixed probabilities `p_m`.

Define two one-step isometries with the same inclusive record POVM:

```text
hidden channel:
  |psi>_{DC}
    -> |psi>_{B} tensor sum_m sqrt(p_m) |m>_R;

export channel:
  |psi>_{DC}
    -> |psi_D>_{R_q} tensor |psi_C>_B
       tensor sum_m sqrt(p_m) |m>_{R_m}.
```

For both channels,

```text
Gamma_m = V_m^\dagger V_m = p_m I_{H_i}.
```

Thus every inclusive rate, detailed-balance factor, and record-number
probability depending only on `Gamma_m` agrees. The diary is absent from the
radiation in the hidden channel and present in the radiation in the export
channel.

Therefore Hawking-rate data or any first-moment calculation of
`V_m^\dagger V_m` cannot imply Page/Hayden-Preskill export. The missing input
is exactly a second-moment or channel-covariance statement about the matrix
elements of `V_m`.

## Analytic Target

Let `P_i = P_{N,E}` project onto the initial fractionated Schwarzschild Matrix
microcanonical sector, and let `P_f(m) = P_{N-q,E-omega_m}` project onto the
remaining black-hole sector after emitting record `m`. The local detachment
operator is

```text
V_{a,m} = P_f(m) O_{a,m} P_i,
```

where `O_{a,m}` deletes or decouples the `O(1)` background links tethering the
D0 site or finite D0 cluster `a`, and projects the separated cluster into the
record bin `m`.

The final black-hole index may still contain information about which tethered
site detached. That source label is part of the final-sector index `beta`, not
part of the outgoing record label `m`.

The physical outgoing channel at that resolution is

```text
V_m = sum_a V_{a,m}.
```

The analytic problem has two moments.

First moment:

```text
(1 / d_i) Tr_i V_m^\dagger V_m
  = Hawking/Matrix inclusive rate for record m.
```

Equivalently,

```text
(1 / d_i) Tr_i V_{a,m}^\dagger V_{b,m}
  is diagonal in a,b at leading order,
```

with the diagonal part giving the BFK incoherent factor of `N`.

Second moment:

```text
M^{(m,n)}_{alpha alpha'; beta beta'; r r'}
  =
  (V_m)_{beta r, alpha}
  overline{(V_n)_{beta' r', alpha'}}.
```

The desired ETH/two-design form is

```text
E_micro M^{(m,n)}_{alpha alpha'; beta beta'; r r'}
  ~ delta_mn delta_rr' delta_alphaalpha' delta_betabeta'
    F_m(E,omega) / d_i,
```

inside fixed energy, D0-number, momentum, and gauge-invariant charge sectors.

Expanded over sources, the object to control is

```text
sum_{a,b}
  (V_{a,m})_{beta r, alpha}
  overline{(V_{b,n})_{beta' r', alpha'}}.
```

The first-moment calculation only needs the trace of this object with
`alpha=alpha'` and `beta=beta'`. Generic information export needs the full
tensor to have the ETH form above.

This gives a concrete test:

```text
Do the connected source-cross terms with a != b self-average strongly enough
that the collective outgoing record behaves like a generic microcanonical
emission operator?
```

One outcome connects Matrix/string evaporation to Page-Hayden-Preskill export.
The other outcome localizes the remaining gravitational ingredient in the
emission channel.

### Statistical-Evaporation Form Of The First Moment

The compound-system form of Hawking thermality is:

```text
Gamma_m(E -> E-omega)
  proportional to
  T_m(omega) rho(E-omega) / rho(E),
```

where `T_m` is the transmission coefficient or greybody factor for the
outgoing channel and `rho` is the internal density of states. For
Schwarzschild scaling,

```text
S(E) = c E^2,
rho(E-omega)/rho(E)
  = exp[-2cE omega + c omega^2]
  = exp[-beta omega + c omega^2].
```

Thus the thermal factor and the leading finite-energy correction are already
the generic first-moment statistics of a dense compound system with
Schwarzschild density of states. This reinforces the split:

```text
state count + channel transmission:
  Hawking-like inclusive spectrum;

channel-amplitude covariance:
  generic information export.
```

This also limits the claim. Ordinary compound systems can have
Hauser-Feshbach rates, Porter-Thomas widths, and open-chaotic loss of
formation memory. The black-hole-specific ingredient is the density of states
and channel spectrum: `S(E) ~ E^2`, Hawking-scale softness, and the Matrix or
holographic mechanism that makes those features arise together.

### Conditional Open-Chaos Bridge

The useful payoff of the chaotic-scattering language is a precise replacement
for the informal phrase "the emission operator is ETH-like."

For Matrix evaporation, the "open" system is an internal Feshbach split of one
unitary BFSS Hilbert space:

```text
P sector:
  clumped black-hole-like Matrix configurations;

Q sector:
  block-separated / flat-direction configurations containing an escaped D0
  cluster and a daughter clump.
```

The continuum language is shorthand for the asymptotic flat-direction sector.
No external bath or nonunitary dynamics is being added.

Let the emitted record be `m`, let `r` label unresolved quantum numbers inside
that record bin, let `alpha` label the initial Matrix black-hole
microcanonical shell, and let `beta` label the daughter black-hole shell. The
detachment map has amplitudes

```text
W^{(m)}_{beta r, alpha}
  = < beta; r_m | V_m | alpha >.
```

The open-chaotic-system ansatz is the covariance statement

```text
E[ W^{(m)}_{beta r, alpha}
   overline{W^{(n)}_{beta' r', alpha'}} ]

  =
  delta_mn delta_rr' delta_beta,beta' delta_alpha,alpha'
  gamma_m(E,omega) / dim H_i
```

up to smooth energy dependence, symmetry blocks, and the usual finite-size
correlations needed by unitarity. This is the Stinespring/channel version of
Porter-Thomas partial-width statistics.

Then:

```text
first moment:
  sum_{m,beta,r} |W^{(m)}_{beta r, alpha}|^2
  gives the inclusive Hawking/BFK rate;

second moment:
  the covariance above makes the emitted record a generic weak export channel
  on the microcanonical shell.
```

With scrambling between emissions, standard decoupling gives
Page/Hayden-Preskill recovery once enough record entropy has been emitted.
So a Matrix-theory proof of this covariance would close the present gap: the
same detachment map would supply Hawking thermodynamics and generic
information export.

This also identifies the possible failure mode. The first moment only fixes
the total width. A channel can have the correct Hawking rate while its
amplitudes are aligned with protected labels, symmetry sectors, or a small
collective subspace. Such a channel would radiate thermally in inclusive
observables and still fail to export arbitrary microstate information at the
Page/Hayden-Preskill level.

### How To Try To Prove Or Disprove It

There are now three concrete attacks, ordered by increasing microscopic
commitment.

```text

   Assume the Matrix black-hole sector is a chaotic compound system and the
```

Matrix configuration tethers distinguishable D0 variables. In the full BFSS
Hilbert space the physical object is a block-splitting or escaping-eigenvalue
wavepacket, together with depletion of the off-diagonal strings connecting it
to the bound block. The source label is therefore a semiclassical doorway
coordinate, not a fundamental gauge label.

The relevant comparison is between:

```text
spreading width:
  rate at which the doorway state mixes into the chaotic compound background;

escape width:
  rate at which the doorway state decays directly into the outgoing channel.
```

If the spreading width is larger than the escape width, the emitted amplitude
samples the compound background before the D0 escapes. Porter-Thomas/open-RMT
statistics are then plausible, and the second-moment bridge can hold.

If the escape width is larger, the channel sees a small set of doorway modes.
Then the inclusive rate can still be correct while the emitted records carry
only doorway-level information. In the strong common-channel limit, the
reaction-theory analogue is superradiant width collectivization: broad
channel-aligned states plus narrow trapped states.

This is a better diagnostic than asking only whether BFSS is chaotic. The
question becomes:

```text
Does the D0 detachment doorway spread through the fractionated Matrix
microcanonical sector before it escapes?
```

That criterion connects the old Dicke/superradiance worry to established
open-chaos language.

The parametric estimate is encouraging. For a Schwarzschild-sized evaporator,
the total emission rate is order one quantum per thermal time. If there are
`S` comparable source sites, the escape width of one local doorway is
approximately

```text
Gamma_escape,one ~ 1 / (S beta).
```

If the local Matrix/link doorway spreads into the compound background on the
thermal or scrambling time,

```text
Gamma_spread ~ 1/beta
  or
Gamma_spread ~ 1/(beta log S),
```

then

```text
Gamma_spread / Gamma_escape,one ~ S
  or
Gamma_spread / Gamma_escape,one ~ S/log S.
```

In that regime the doorway should equilibrate inside the black-hole sector
long before it escapes. The open-RMT covariance would then be the natural
statistical-reaction limit. The hard Matrix question is whether the
link-deletion/escaping-eigenvalue doorway really has this spreading width in
the fractionated Schwarzschild sector.

This is where existing BFSS real-time work becomes directly useful. Numerical
studies of BFSS chaos and thermalization already measure Lyapunov growth,
entanglement growth, and quasinormal ringing; the quasinormal decay time is a
candidate proxy for the internal doorway-spreading time. If that time is
thermal or faster while per-source emission is suppressed by `1/S`, the
doorway condition is parametrically satisfied.

Minimal doorway model:

```text
H = E_d |d><d|
    + H_bg
    + sum_i (v_i |d><i| + h.c.),

W = sqrt(gamma_escape) |channel><d|.
```

Here `|d>` is the simple detachment doorway and `H_bg` is the chaotic
black-hole background. The doorway strength spreads over background
eigenstates with width

```text
Gamma_down ~ 2 pi rho(E) <|v|^2>,
```

where `rho(E)` is the background density of states. A compound eigenstate
`|n>` couples to the channel with amplitude

```text
W_n = sqrt(gamma_escape) <d|n>.
```

When the doorway strength is spread over many compound levels, the overlaps
`<d|n>` have Porter-Thomas statistics within the strength-function envelope.
This is precisely the desired channel-amplitude randomness. When the doorway
remains concentrated in a few eigenstates, the channel retains doorway memory.

For information export the one-doorway display is only a warm-up. The real
Feshbach object is the transition matrix

```text
W_{c alpha} = < c | H | alpha >,
```

where `alpha` is an initial black-hole compound eigenstate and the exit
channel `c` includes the daughter black-hole state plus the emitted record:

```text
c = (m, beta, r).
```

Equivalently, each exit channel defines a doorway vector in the initial shell,

```text
|d_c> = P_i H |c>,

W_{c alpha} = <d_c|alpha>.
```

The desired second moment is therefore a statement about the Gram matrix of
these channel-doorway vectors after chaotic spreading:

```text
E_alpha[ W_{c alpha} overline{W_{c' alpha}} ]
  ~ <d_c|d_c'> / dim H_i.
```

The export bridge needs two facts:

```text
1. spreading:
   the chaotic initial shell makes <d_c|alpha> behave like random eigenvector
   components inside the strength-function envelope;

2. channel isotropy:
   the Gram matrix <d_c|d_c'> is close to diagonal, or at least has high
   participation, across the full exit-channel labels c=(m,beta,r).
```

The earlier source-width matrix `G_ab` diagnoses the second fact in the
semiclassical source basis. The detachment correlator diagnoses the first.

This separation is necessary. Perfect spreading alone is insufficient. If all
exit channels share the same doorway vector,

```text
|d_c> = u_c |d>,
```

then

```text
W_{c alpha} = u_c <d|alpha>
```

has Porter-Thomas fluctuations in `alpha`, but the channel matrix has rank one
in `c`. The radiation can have the right total width and chaotic-looking
partial-width fluctuations while exporting only the single collective label
associated with `|d>`. Generic export requires spreading plus a high-rank
channel-doorway Gram matrix.

Thus the Matrix calculation can be phrased as:

```text
identify the channel-doorway vectors |d_c> for D0 detachment;
estimate their spreading width Gamma_down in the fractionated sector;
compare Gamma_down with its direct escape width;
check whether the channel-doorway Gram matrix has high participation across
the full exit-channel labels c=(m,beta,r).
```

Operationally, `Gamma_down` is the width of the detachment-operator strength
function:

```text
A_detach(omega)
  = sum_n |<n|O_detach|d>|^2 delta(omega - (E_n-E_d)),
```

or equivalently the decay rate of the microcanonical correlator

```text
C(t) = < O_detach^\dagger(t) O_detach(0) >_microcanonical.
```

A fast decay of `C(t)` on the thermal or quasinormal timescale is evidence
that the doorway spreads through the Matrix sector before emission. Persistent
oscillations, scars, or a few sharp peaks in `A_detach` would signal a
structured doorway and weaken the export bridge.

This is the same object that appears in ETH/Fermi-golden-rule language: a
golden-rule rate probes the smooth envelope of off-diagonal matrix elements of
the perturbing operator. For the detachment problem that envelope is the
doorway strength function. It supplies the spreading/variance half of the
bridge. The channel-doorway Gram matrix supplies the independent participation
half.

The common-channel/Dicke risk is the spectrum of the inclusive source-width
matrix. If `a,b` label local detachment doorways, define

```text
G_ab = sum_{m,beta,r} W^{(m)}_{beta r,a}
       overline{W^{(m)}_{beta r,b}}.
```

Many comparable eigenvalues of `G` mean many independent source doorways feed
the radiation channel. A single large eigenvalue, or a few large eigenvalues,
means the outgoing channel has selected superradiant collective combinations
and left many trapped combinations. The first moment sees only `Tr G`; export
still requires covariance across compound eigenstates. Low participation is a
warning that the rate may be dominated by a collective doorway, with only a
small number of effective source directions. This is the reaction-theory
version of the boundary-saturation invariant.

The sum over `beta,r` is essential. Summing only over the few far-field
radiation modes measures the radiation coherence matrix and is capped by the
instantaneous mode bottleneck. The source-width matrix asks how many
black-hole doorway directions participate in the full transition, including
the daughter black-hole state.

This is a diagnostic gate, not the final export theorem. The export theorem
is still the decoupling statement for the full isometry

```text
V: H_i -> H_f tensor H_R.
```

Doorway spreading and high width participation are evidence that `V` is in the
right statistical class. They do not replace the final covariance or
decoupling check.

### What Existing Matrix Scalings Can And Cannot Estimate

The BFK/BFKS scaling data estimate the escape side of the doorway comparison.
For an entropy-sized set of comparable D0 source sites,

```text
S ~ N_sources,
Gamma_total ~ one Hawking quantum per beta,
Gamma_escape,one ~ Gamma_total / N_sources ~ 1/(S beta).
```

This follows from the same incoherent-source logic that gives the BFK factor
of `N`: many source sites contribute to the inclusive Hawking rate, while a
fixed site has an `O(1)` local liberation amplitude in the Hawking-scaled
variables.

Two quantities are not fixed by these scalings:

```text
1. Gamma_down:
   the spreading width of the local block-splitting / link-deletion doorway
   inside the fractionated Matrix sector;

2. participation(G):
   the rank or inverse-participation of the channel-doorway Gram matrix after
   summing over full exit labels c=(m,beta,r).
```

The first can be probed by the microcanonical detachment correlator
`<O_detach^\dagger(t) O_detach(0)>`. Existing BFSS thermalization and
quasinormal-mode results are evidence for fast relaxation of some
perturbations, but they do not identify the D0-detachment doorway.

The second cannot be inferred from the total rate or from far-field flux
modes. It requires the daughter-state-resolved transition amplitudes, or an
argument that the channel-doorway vectors are isotropic in the Matrix
microcanonical shell.

So the strongest parametric statement currently justified is:

```text
If the detachment doorway relaxes on a thermal, quasinormal, or scrambling
timescale, then Gamma_down / Gamma_escape,one is parametrically large:

  Gamma_down / Gamma_escape,one ~ S
  or
  Gamma_down / Gamma_escape,one ~ S/log S.

This makes the spreading gate plausible.

The Gram-participation gate remains independent.
```

### Gram-Participation Non-Identifiability

The channel-doorway participation is invisible to the inclusive rate. Let the
full exit-channel label be `c=(m,beta,r)` and let the doorway matrix be

```text
D_{c alpha} = <d_c|alpha>.
```

Fix the same total width:

```text
Tr D^\dagger D = Gamma_total.
```

Two extreme choices agree on this first moment:

```text
isotropic:
  D has many independent rows with comparable norms;
  D^\dagger D ~ (Gamma_total/dim H_i) I;
  D D^\dagger has high participation.

aligned:
  D_{c alpha} = u_c v_alpha;
  D^\dagger D = Gamma_total |v><v|;
  D D^\dagger = Gamma_total |u><u|;
  participation is one.
```

Both can be normalized to the same `Gamma_total`. The isotropic case is the
random-doorway benchmark. The aligned case is the superradiant/common-doorway
counterexample. Therefore:

```text
total Hawking width + large Hilbert-space dimension
  does not imply high channel-doorway participation.
```

The Matrix calculation must use structural information about the detachment
operator, such as locality in the BFKS background, gauge-invariant
block-splitting structure, or random-matrix/ETH behavior of the projected
operators. Dimensional counting alone cannot close the Gram gate.

### Matrix Factorization Is Dynamical

The Matrix route also sharpens the factorization issue. The split

```text
H_i -> H_f tensor H_R
```

is not a fundamental tensor factorization of local bulk regions. In BFSS it is
an asymptotic block decomposition:

```text
clumped noncommuting block
  -> daughter clump block + separated D0/short-string block,
```

with off-diagonal strings between the blocks becoming heavy along the flat
direction. Radiation factorization is therefore a late-time/asymptotic
property of block separation. The detachment operator is the dynamical map
that creates the approximate factorization used in Page bookkeeping.

This helps the demarcation. A factorized Hamiltonian model supplies
`H_B tensor H_R` as an input. Matrix theory can, in principle, derive that
split from gauge/matrix dynamics. The same detachment operator must then do
three jobs:

```text
create the asymptotic radiation block;
give the Hawking first moment;
have the second-moment covariance needed for information export.
```

### Structural Support From The BFKS Background

BFKS/BFKS II provide one possible reason for high Gram participation. In the
semiclassical Boltzmann-D0 picture, the metastable Matrix background tethers
distinguishable D0 variables to different background cells. A local detachment
operator deletes the `O(1)` links around one cell. If different cells define
approximately orthogonal doorway wavepackets, then

```text
<d_{a,c}|d_{b,c'}> ~ 0,  a != b,
```

up to overlaps set by the background correlation length and gauge projection.
In that semiclassical regime, the source-doorway Gram matrix has participation
of order the number of active tethered cells:

```text
participation(G_source) ~ S.
```

This is exactly the structural ingredient missing from a generic rate
calculation. The BFK incoherent sum gives the trace; the BFKS tethered
background can supply approximate orthogonality of the local source
doorways.

The caveat is also clear. In the full gauge-invariant Hilbert space, a labeled
source site is a coordinate on a semiclassical background. If gauge projection
or collective radiation coupling erases the source distinction before the
daughter black-hole state is included, the Gram matrix can collapse toward the
aligned-doorway limit. The correct participation test must use physical
block-splitting/daughter-state labels, not bare gauge labels.

### Conditional ETH Estimate For The Spreading Gate

The dense spectrum makes the spreading gate less mysterious once the
detachment operator is assumed to be ETH-ordinary. For a chaotic
microcanonical shell with entropy `S`, an ordinary few-body or finite-rank
operator has ETH-sized off-diagonal matrix elements

```text
|O_alpha beta|^2 ~ e^{-S} f_O(E,omega)^2.
```

The golden-rule spreading width of a doorway created by `O` is

```text
Gamma_down(omega)
  ~ 2 pi rho(E+omega) |O_alpha beta|^2
  ~ 2 pi f_O(E,omega)^2,
```

since `rho ~ e^S` in the same shell. The exponential density cancels the
ETH suppression. Thus a detachment doorway fails to spread before escape only
if the relevant ETH envelope is parametrically small, if selection rules
isolate a small block, or if the detachment operator is scarred/structured
instead of ordinary in the chaotic Matrix sector.

In this form, the missing Matrix input is:

```text
The gauge-invariant block-splitting / link-deletion operator obeys ordinary
ETH in the fractionated Schwarzschild Matrix sector, with an envelope on the
thermal or quasinormal scale.
```

The surrounding literature makes this input plausible but does not prove it
for the needed operator. ETH has been numerically tested in constrained
Hilbert spaces and lattice gauge theories, including off-diagonal matrix
elements and some nonlocal operators. Those results support the idea that
gauge constraints do not automatically block ETH. They do not identify the
BFSS block-splitting / D0-detachment operator or its strength function.

The BFSS real-time literature also supports the scale, with a caveat. It
finds Lyapunov growth, entanglement saturation, and quasinormal ringing in
thermal BFSS approximations, with quasinormal decay as the shortest observed
timescale in that study. This is evidence that Matrix thermal dynamics can tie
local relaxation and chaos to the temperature. It still leaves the
operator-specific detachment correlator as the missing object.

Because detachment changes the block structure and D0 number of the clump, the
ETH statement is a transition-operator version of ETH:

```text
O_detach:
  H_{N,E}^{clump}
    -> H_{N-q,E-omega}^{daughter} tensor H_q^{escape}.
```

The relevant random-matrix claim is not diagonal ETH for an observable inside
one shell. It is that the projected transition operator

```text
P_{N-q,E-omega}^{daughter} O_detach P_{N,E}^{clump}
```

has smooth envelope and random components between the two chaotic shells,
after exact charges and gauge constraints are fixed. This is the Matrix
version of a compound-nucleus transition operator.

If this input holds, then

```text
Gamma_down ~ 1/beta
  or at worst 1/(beta log S),
```

and the earlier comparison with

```text
Gamma_escape,one ~ 1/(S beta)
```

gives a parametrically large spreading-to-escape ratio. The remaining
independent gate is the channel-doorway Gram participation.

Conditional doorway bridge:

```text
Assume:
  1. the fractionated Matrix shell is chaotic within the relevant symmetry
     block;
  2. each D0 exit channel c=(m,beta,r) defines a channel-doorway vector |d_c>
     in the initial shell;
  3. the doorway strength functions spread over K >> 1 compound eigenstates
     in the Hawking energy window;
  4. direct escape is weak compared with spreading:
       Gamma_escape,one << Gamma_down;
  5. the doorway Gram matrix <d_c|d_c'> is diagonal or high-participation
     within the resolved channel labels, after exact symmetry sectors are
     separated;
  6. no conserved charge or selection rule correlates the emitted record with
     a protected microstate label.

Then:
  the channel amplitudes from compound eigenstates to the emitted D0 record
  have Porter-Thomas/open-chaotic covariance inside the strength envelope.
```

Proof sketch:

```text
Diagonalize the initial Matrix black-hole Hamiltonian before coupling strongly
to the continuum. Since each channel doorway |d_c> is spread over K chaotic
compound eigenstates, the overlaps <d_c|alpha> behave as random eigenvector
components subject to a smooth strength-function envelope. Their two-point
covariance is controlled by the doorway Gram matrix <d_c|d_c'>. If that Gram
matrix is diagonal or high-participation in the full exit-channel label
c=(m,beta,r), the Stinespring matrix has the open-chaotic covariance needed
for random-channel export. Weak escape prevents continuum collectivization
from selecting a small superradiant subspace before compounding.
```

With repeated emissions and internal re-scrambling, this is the statistical
input needed by the decoupling/Page-Hayden-Preskill export argument.

Random-doorway benchmark:

```text

Assume these vectors are independent isotropic vectors in the initial shell,
with smooth channel-dependent norms gamma_c.

Then:
  E[D D^\dagger]_{cc'} = gamma_c delta_cc',
  E[D^\dagger D] = (sum_c gamma_c / dim H_i) I.
```

For channel number and shell dimension large enough, standard matrix
concentration gives small off-diagonal Gram elements and a smooth inclusive
operator `D^\dagger D`. This benchmark realizes both requirements:

```text

  fixed by Tr D^\dagger D = sum_c gamma_c;

export covariance:
  supplied by isotropic channel-doorway vectors.
```

## Exhaustion Checkpoint

Closed or no longer worth re-searching broadly:

```text
Page/recovery once a generic channel is supplied:
  standard quantum information.

Hawking-like thermality from a dense spectrum:
  standard statistical evaporation via rho(E-omega)/rho(E).

Matrix/BFSS ingredients for state count, softness, D0 escape, and Page
bookkeeping:
  present across BFKS/KS/BFK, BFSS evaporation, and black-zero-brane Page
curve work.

Generic chaotic-scattering machinery:
  present in compound-nucleus/open-quantum-chaos literature.
```

Still genuinely open in the searched literature:

```text
Apply the statistical-reaction machinery to the BFSS/Matrix D0 detachment
vertex and test whether it supplies the second-moment covariance required for
generic information export.

The required test has four gates:
  doorway spreading through the chaotic Matrix sector;
  high participation of the channel-doorway Gram matrix across full exit
  channels c=(m,beta,r);
  high participation after projection to the accessible radiation record;
  positive entropy rate for record histories under repeated emission.
```

The same gates appear in three languages:

```text
Reaction theory:
  spreading width, partial-width covariance, and channel balance.

ETH/Fermi golden rule:
  detachment strength function plus correlations among the projected
  transition operators and their resolvable record bins.

Quantum information:
  Stinespring covariance, accessible Kraus histories, and decoupling.
```

This convergence is the current reason to stop broad reframing. The remaining
unknown is the Matrix detachment dynamics.

The remaining work has narrowed to four quantitative questions:

```text
1. Doorway spreading:
   Does the gauge-invariant D0-detachment transition operator obey ETH
   between the clumped Matrix shell and the daughter-plus-escape shell, with a
   thermal/quasinormal-scale strength function?

2. Width participation:
   Does the physical channel-doorway Gram matrix have high participation
   across full exit channels c=(m,beta,r), or does it collapse onto a small
   superradiant/common-doorway subspace?

3. Accessible-record balance:
   Does the participation survive after coarse-graining to the radiation
   labels available to the recovery problem?

4. Temporal export:
   Do repeated emissions and daughter reprocessing give
   h_eff(k) = k^{-1} log N_eff(k) bounded away from zero?
```

Current status of the gates:

```text
escape side:
  BFK/BFKS scaling supports Gamma_escape,one ~ 1/(S beta).

participation side:
  BFKS tethering gives a plausible semiclassical source-orthogonality
  mechanism, participation ~ S, provided physical daughter-state labels retain
  the block-splitting distinction.

spreading side:
  BFSS thermalization and quasinormal results suggest fast relaxation for
  some perturbations. The remaining question is whether the specific
  gauge-invariant D0-detachment/link-deletion operator obeys ordinary ETH in
  the fractionated Matrix sector. If it does, the dense spectrum cancels the
  ETH matrix-element suppression and gives a thermal-scale spreading width.
```

If both answers are favorable, the Matrix substrate supplies the bridge from
thermodynamics to information export. If either answer fails, the demarcation
is also sharp: Matrix evaporation can reproduce inclusive Hawking physics
while generic export remains an additional statistical or gravitational
assumption.

Known failure mode:

```text
All exit channels can share a common doorway vector. Then the doorway can have
Porter-Thomas fluctuations across compound eigenstates while the channel
matrix remains low-rank in exit-channel space. This gives chaotic-looking
widths and the correct total rate without generic export.
```
