# Standalone-Result and Literature-Overlap Passes

Date: 2026-07-16

Status: complete for the bounded external-readiness pass. This note records
the claims that survive comparison, the claims that must be downgraded, and
the scope limits that must travel with each result.

The pass is not an exhaustive review of quantum-gravity literature. It is the
specified comparison against the nearest mechanisms and the standard
mathematical tools named in the endpoint gate.

## Result A — unified evaporator

### Comparison

Gyongyosi, Hollowood, Kumar, Legramandi, and Talwar, *The Holographic Map of
an Evaporating Black Hole*, arXiv:2301.08362, already construct an effective
model-level dynamical bridge. Their nested random-unitary map incorporates
energy conservation and thermal Hawking radiation and supports Page/QES and
reconstruction analyses.

That paper therefore occupies the broad claim that an effective evaporating
model can connect thermal radiation dynamics with information recovery. The
project must not present that broad bridge as its distinctive discovery.

### Surviving statement

The defensible A claim is narrower and control-model specific:

> A finite sector-isometry process places the thermodynamic package and the
> information diagnostics in the same evolving state: near-thermal hard
> radiation, negative heat capacity, an `S ~ E^2` sector-counting window, an
> accelerating power window, shrinking core support, a Page-like radiation
> turnover, and early/late correlations. A matched linear-mass control has a
> similar hard spectrum but does not show the same acceleration.

The distinctive content is the controlled thermodynamic contrast and its
coexistence with the information diagnostics, not the generic existence of a
Page curve, thermal radiation, or a dynamical bridge.

### Required qualification

The construction supplies finite sectors, an equilibration rule, and an
isometry. It does not derive the black-hole area law, a gravitational
radiation algebra, or an autonomous microscopic black-hole Hamiltonian. The
external-safe label is therefore **integrated non-gravitational control-model
result**, not **first model of evaporating black-hole information flow**.

## Result B — composable temporal access

### Comparison

The proof is the standard hybrid/telescoping argument for sequential quantum
strategies. Insert the actual map in one time slot at a time, use contractivity
of later channels and partial trace, and apply the triangle inequality. This
is structurally covered by the general theory of quantum strategies and
combs in Gutoski and Watrous, by the strategy-distance formulation of Gutoski,
and by the channel-continuity literature initiated by Kretschmann,
Schlingemann, and Werner.

### Verdict

The inequality itself is **not a new generic comb-continuity theorem**. It
should be presented as a composable temporal-access application/corollary with
a useful operational formulation:

* arbitrary finite sequential maps with changing shell dimensions and shared
  memory;
* defects evaluated only on hybrid-reachable states rather than on an
  unnecessarily global norm;
* comparison to a fully diary-blind process;
* the recovery converse that orthogonal diary recovery with error `alpha`
  requires cumulative defect at least `1 - alpha`.

The result is worth retaining because it gives a compact necessary condition
for temporal access in an evaporation comb, and because the exact blind-pump
and correlation-only controls make the distinction between thermality,
multitime correlation, and recoverability concrete. The external-safe label
is **application-level temporal-access theorem**.

## Result C — static-inference no-go family

### Comparison

The individual ingredients are standard: nonuniqueness of interaction/Kraus
factorizations, invariant channel/Choi descriptions, Gaussian moment
nonidentifiability, active Bogoliubov channels, and finite-sample precision
costs. The novelty claim must not be that any one of these mathematical facts
is new.

### Surviving statement

The defensible C claim is a packaged no-go for a proposed certificate:

> Aggregate Hawking-like flux, calibrated response, photon counting, Gaussian
> delay correlations, and feasible stationary tomography do not, in general,
> identify a hidden microscopic source-participation count.

The package has independent failure modes: source-list refactorization changes
raw source rank; aggregate Gaussian data admit arbitrary hidden decompositions;
signed sectors can cancel in coarse observables; an active Gaussian channel can
match the static Hawking package without passive starvation; and the precision
needed for high-rank inference can consume a substantial fraction of one
evaporation history.

The invariant replacement is a coupling-weighted radiation process—e.g. a
channel/Choi spectrum or a temporal coupling orbit—not an arbitrary list of
microscopic source terms.

### Static-patch/Banks paragraph

The Banks finite-static-patch perspective strengthens the scope discipline but
does not reverse the no-go. A finite static-patch state count and the resulting
limits on local measurement precision can constrain the total Hilbert-space
story while leaving multiple microscopic theories indistinguishable to the
allowed local measurements. Thus “finite static-patch algebra” is not by
itself a certificate for a particular source decomposition, diary-dependent
radiation map, or recovery protocol. This is an inference about how that
perspective bears on C, not a claim that Banks proves the C theorem.

The external-safe label is **no-go family against static exterior source
certification**, with every passive or precision statement carrying its stated
model assumptions.

## Result D — strict memory-burden prototype

### Scope audit

The local calculation is correctly scoped to the canonical strict
Dvali–Eisemann–Michel–Zell prototype (arXiv:2006.00011) and its declared
master-radiation line. The later black-hole/soliton memory-burden paper
(arXiv:2405.13117) supports the post-burden slowdown context, but does not
replace the strict Hamiltonian calculation with a recovery-fidelity,
decoupling, or channel-rank theorem. The Dvali–Panchenko critical-condensate
paper (arXiv:1601.01329) supplies relevant collective-mode/readout precedent,
so collective coupling itself must not be claimed as new.

### Surviving statement

For the strict prototype and the specified resolved line, the program finds:

```text
entropy-sized assisted memory degeneracy;
one bright source direction;
fixed-sector diary export blocked by conserved memory occupation;
g2(0) = 1 - 1/S;
emission/absorption = 1 - 1/S.
```

This separates degeneracy, source rank, and diary latency. It is not a
detector-independent statement about realistic Hawking radiation. Continuum
outgoing modes, rescattering, pair annihilation, exchange, dressing, or extra
radiation vertices require separate calculations.

The external-safe label is **strict-prototype adjudication**, not a verdict on
the whole N-portrait or memory-burden literature.

## Final readiness decision

A–D are worth keeping and are now usable as internally defensible results,
provided the labels above are used. The passes do not justify reopening the
DSSYK successor yet; that remains a separate theory-specific bridge question.
