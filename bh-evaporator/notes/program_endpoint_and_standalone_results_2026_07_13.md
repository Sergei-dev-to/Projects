# Program Endpoint, Results, and Handoff

Date: 2026-07-13

Status: final program-level wrap-up. This note supersedes earlier steering
documents where they prescribe additional control-model, demarcation, or BFSS
work as a required continuation.

## The question that started the program

The project began with a control experiment. Could one coherent
non-gravitational quantum process reproduce the exterior package associated
with black-hole evaporation?

```text
negative heat capacity and heating during shrinkage;
near-thermal emitted quanta;
accelerating power in a Schwarzschild-like window;
a shrinking internal state space;
global unitarity;
a Page-like radiation-entropy turnover;
information stored and recoverable in the radiation record.
```

If this package could be realized without gravity, its internal logic would
not by itself identify a uniquely gravitational mechanism. Gravity would
still have to explain why an actual black hole realizes the required state
count, observables, and dynamics.

## The answer

The answer to the control question is yes. The unified sector-isometry model
places the thermodynamic and information diagnostics in one evolving state.
Its hard radiation is nearly thermal, its Schwarzschild-like state count gives
negative heat capacity and an accelerating power window, its internal support
shrinks, and its accumulated radiation shows a Page-like turnover and
early/late correlations.

The comparative literature pass also found that the broader bridge from
state-dependent radiation dynamics to reconstruction is already realized at
the effective-model level. Random-unitary, operator-growth, replica/Petz,
equilibrium, and fixed-Hamiltonian many-body models supply different versions
of this connection. The bridge remains important, and the broad model-level
question now has an answer from the project and the literature together.

## What "complete" means

The completed object is this research program: its control experiment, its
comparison of existing frameworks, its identifiability tests, and its decision
about what further work belongs inside the same effort. The program has an
answer to the question it began with and no remaining calculation that must be
performed to make that answer meaningful.

The black-hole information problem as a field is not complete. A fundamental
theory still has to derive the state space, radiation observables, emission
dynamics, constraints, and interior of an actual black hole. Those problems
motivate successor projects with different technical commitments.

## What the program learned

The attempted boundary between “gravity” and “quantum information” was too
dependent on the chosen description to serve as the final result. The durable
replacement is a set of four obligations for any microscopic evaporation
model:

1. **Thermodynamic obligation:** derive the state count and its energy/charge
   dependence.
2. **Observable-structure obligation:** identify the accessible radiation
   algebra, its sectors, and its complement.
3. **Dynamical obligation:** derive the emission process and show how
   private-state differences enter the emitted record over time.
4. **Consistency obligation:** maintain unitarity, conservation laws,
   shrinking capacity, and a complete disposition of partners or other hidden
   systems.

The comparison also separated four information claims:

```text
destination       where the information finally resides;
dynamical access  when the emitted history begins to depend on it;
algebraic access  whether the radiation can reconstruct it in principle;
recovery          whether a specified decoder returns it with controlled error.
```

This vocabulary prevents a Page curve, an island, a state-dependent emission,
and a usable decoder from being treated as interchangeable achievements.

## Retained result B: a composable temporal-access obstruction

Primary record:

- `q2_composable_diary_access_theorem.md`
- `shrinking_shell_diary_access_result.md`
- `microcanonical_coded_pump_construction.md`
- `temporal_access_necessary_sufficient_synthesis.md`

Result:

> For a memoryful sequential emitter, the full-record distance from a
> diary-blind comparison process is bounded by the sum of the one-step defects
> evaluated on the hybrid-reachable states. Accurate recovery therefore
> requires an order-one cumulative departure from diary blindness.

The blind pump and correlation-only code are exact controls. They show that
thermal emissions and partner production can remain diary-blind, and that
private information can reside entirely in multitime correlations.

The bound is necessary rather than sufficient. A large cumulative defect need
not point in enough independent directions to support recovery. Sufficiency
requires additional mixing, decoupling, temporal-design, or ETH assumptions.
The bounded comparison against standard hybrid and process-continuity bounds
is complete: the telescoping inequality is standard at the generic comb level,
while the diary-blind evaporation comparison, hybrid-reachable defects, and
recovery converse are the retained application-level content. See
`standalone_result_passes_2026_07_16.md` for the external-use wording.

## Retained result C: limits of static exterior inference

Primary record:

- `static_source_rank_certificate_tombstone.md`
- `source_gram_invariance_audit.md`
- `signed_cancellation_and_gram_tail_result.md`
- `anomalous_parametric_channel_result.md`
- `delay_correlation_nonidentifiability_result.md`
- `certificate_time_budget_result.md`

Result:

> Aggregate Hawking-like flux, calibrated response, photon counting, Gaussian
> delay correlations, and feasible stationary tomography do not determine a
> hidden microscopic source-participation count in general.

The route fails for several independent reasons. The raw source Gram
participation changes under interaction refactorization; aggregate Gaussian
data admit hidden decompositions; signed sectors can cancel; an active
Bogoliubov channel can reproduce the static Hawking package without passive
starvation; and high-rank precision can consume an order-one fraction of a
single evaporation history.

Narrow passive diagnostics remain valid under their stated assumptions. The
result closes the proposed general certificate while preserving those
conditional model tests. It also explains why the physical radiation process,
rather than an arbitrary list of microscopic source terms, is the appropriate
invariant object.

## Retained result D: adjudication of the strict memory-burden prototype

Primary record:

- `prototype_m0_m1_results.md`
- `prototype_m3_discriminator_table.md`
- `asymmetry_backreaction_escape_result.md`

Within the canonical strict N-portrait/memory-burden Hamiltonian, the program
finds:

```text
entropy-sized assisted memory degeneracy;
one bright source direction at the specified master-radiation line;
fixed-sector diary export blocked by conserved memory occupation;
for the resolved strict-prototype mode, g2(0) = 1 - 1/S;
for the specified transition, emission/absorption = 1 - 1/S.
```

The important conceptual result is the separation of three properties that
are easily conflated: a large memory degeneracy, many independent active
radiation sources, and rapid export of a private state. The strict Hamiltonian
has the first without the latter two in its master-radiation record.

This is a result about the declared prototype and its chosen resolved channel.
It is not yet a detector-independent statement about realistic Hawking
radiation. A continuum of outgoing modes, rescattering, pair annihilation,
exchange, dressing, or additional radiation vertices can change the answer.
Each variant requires a new model calculation; the strict result does not
silently include them.

## External-readiness pass (2026-07-16)

The bounded A--D overlap and scope pass is recorded in
`standalone_result_passes_2026_07_16.md`. Its conclusions are:

- A survives as an integrated non-gravitational control-model result. The
  distinctive content is the controlled thermodynamic package in the same
  state as the information diagnostics, not the generic thermality/Page/QES
  bridge already present in nearby effective models.
- B is an application-level temporal-access theorem. Its telescoping proof is
  standard comb/hybrid continuity; its value is the diary-blind evaporation
  comparison, hybrid-reachable defects, and recovery converse.
- C is a no-go family against static exterior source certification. Its
  individual ingredients are standard, while the independent counterexample
  package and the evaporation precision budget are the retained contribution.
- D is a strict-prototype adjudication. It does not cover the full
  N-portrait/memory-burden corpus or realistic continuum Hawking radiation.

These labels are the conditions for external use of A--D. The DSSYK bridge
remains a parked successor question rather than an uncompleted obligation of
this program.

## Supporting closures

Several attractive ideas were clarified without becoming separate program
goals:

- The approximate capacity cut follows conditionally from standard
  correctability once the subsystem, code, and error hypotheses are stated.
- Perturbative gravitational dressing forces access to asymptotic charge
  information but does not force arbitrary fixed-charge payload recovery at
  that order.
- Type-II gravitational algebras and physical clocks make relational
  observables and entropy well posed. Algebra type alone does not establish
  diary dependence in a radiation history.
- Page behavior, Hayden--Preskill recovery, island reconstruction, and explicit
  dynamical bridges answer different information questions.

## Deliberately parked branches

The observation-facing work remains part of the record. The signed
starvation/asymmetry diagnostics are conditional phenomenology for declared
passive model classes; the no-go results prevent using them as a universal
microscopic certificate. The frozen-routing witness is a developed
experimental control proposal with its own feasibility and collaboration
requirements. Neither branch is needed to complete the central control-model
and comparative argument.

The proposed generic dressed pump is also parked. A freely chosen observable
algebra would predetermine whether it exposes only charge metadata or a full
private payload. The question becomes physical only in a named gravitational
theory whose algebra and dressing are derived.

## Successor research

Several major questions remain open in quantum gravity:

1. derive a gauge-invariant, state-dependent radiation process from BFSS or
   another top-down microscopic theory;
2. explain replica-wormhole factorization within a single microscopic theory;
3. determine the exact fixed-charge information content of a gravitational
   boundary algebra;
4. derive ordinary nonextremal black-hole microstates and the coefficient
   `A/4G`;
5. explain the interior and the endpoint of complete evaporation.

These active research directions lie beyond the completed program. Reopening
should begin from a theory-specific question, a calculable physical observable,
and a reason the answer would add a gravitational ingredient that the existing
effective bridges do not already supply.
