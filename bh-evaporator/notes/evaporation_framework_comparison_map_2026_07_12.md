Date: 2026-07-12

Updated: 2026-07-13 after adding the effective dynamical-bridge literature,
the strict memory-burden prototype, and the program-endpoint decision.

Status: final comparative synthesis for the completed control-model program.
It should be read before the older demarcation ledgers and conjecture notes.

## The result in brief

The black-hole information problem is not waiting for one missing proof. It is
covered by several powerful results that answer different questions.

Hawking explains the thermal flux but, at leading semiclassical order, not the
fate of a particular quantum state. Page explains the entropy expected from a
typical pure state once a black-hole/radiation split and random-state model are
supplied. Hayden and Preskill explain rapid recovery once an old black hole is
supplied as a sufficiently scrambling encoder. Islands and replica wormholes
go further than an entropy bookkeeping device: in controlled gravitational
models they identify a transition in the radiation's entanglement wedge and
therefore establish in-principle, code-subspace reconstruction of interior
information. The island rule alone does not provide a microscopic,
time-ordered account of which emitted degrees of freedom carry which part of a
chosen input.

Effective bridge models do provide such accounts. Chaotic operator-gas and
equilibrium-approximation models derive Page behavior, diary transfer, and
recovery diagnostics from explicit effective dynamics. Most directly, the
block-random-unitary holographic map builds a temporal sequence of thermal,
energy-conserving emissions and derives both the QES formula and
entanglement-wedge reconstruction in the same model. The bridge between
dynamics and reconstruction therefore exists at the controlled effective-model
level.

Fixed-Hamiltonian many-body models close part of the remaining distance as well.
An SYK system coupled to a Majorana-chain bath exhibits a Page transition and
retrieval of a control bit, with the late saddle interpreted as a replica
wormhole and the bath wedge acquiring an island. Equilibrium methods also show
how replica-wormhole formulas can arise for a fixed Hamiltonian without an
ensemble average.

Matrix theory supplies a plausible microscopic evaporation event -- a D0-brane
escaping a bound state -- and reproduces important thermodynamic features. The
available BFSS Page-curve calculation does not yet derive the complete
state-dependent radiation channel from the BFSS Hamiltonian. Perturbative
gravitational dressing forces asymptotic information about total charges, but
existing gravitational-splitting results allow private states with the same
charges to remain indistinguishable outside. Strong holography-of-information
claims challenge that conclusion beyond the perturbative setting; the dispute
is real and should not be replaced by a generic toy pump.

The project's strict memory-burden calculation supplies a named-model
adjudication. The prototype has entropy-sized assisted memory degeneracy, yet
its Hawking-scale flux is carried by one collectively enhanced source and its
fixed-sector private information remains outside the master radiation record.
This conclusion is specific to the stated Hamiltonian and can change when new
exchange or release channels are added.

Finally, the type-III-to-type-II algebra story is genuinely connected to
clocks, gravitational constraints, and the existence of a trace suitable for
generalized entropy. It is not presently a theorem about when an evaporating
radiation record becomes sensitive to a diary. Evaporation can itself supply a
physical clock without thereby supplying a decoder or a microscopic emission
map.

For a real black hole, a top-down quantum-gravity version of the bridge remains
open:

> From a fundamental gravitational theory, derive the physical radiation
> sector, its constrained observables, and its state-dependent emission
> dynamics, then show how they realize the established reconstruction picture.

Destination, entropy turnover, conditional recovery, and the model-level
dynamical bridge already have substantial answers. A fundamental theory must
still explain why an actual black hole has the required state space,
observable radiation, and emission dynamics. That is an active quantum-gravity
frontier. Pursuing it would begin a theory-specific successor to the original
control-model project.

## Four questions, not three

The destination/access/recovery trichotomy remains useful, but the islands pass
shows that its middle term must be divided.

```text
destination:
  where the information must ultimately reside;

dynamical access:
  when and through which emitted observables the radiation process begins to
  depend on a chosen private input;

algebraic access:
  whether that input is encoded in the accessible radiation algebra or
  entanglement wedge, so that a reconstruction exists in principle;

recovery:
  whether a decoder, decoupling theorem, or explicit reconstruction returns
  the input with controlled error.
```

Algebraic access can be established without a microscopic movie of dynamical
access. A quantum error-correcting code makes the distinction familiar. One can
prove that a logical qubit is reconstructible from a region without knowing a
natural local circuit that transported the qubit there. Conversely, a detector
can show weak state dependence without having enough of the record to recover
the state.

means inferable from the declared accessible observations, not merely visible

## Calibration rows

### Hawking: the furnace is calculated, not the message

Hawking's calculation begins with quantum fields on a geometry formed by
collapse. Positive-frequency modes before collapse become a mixture of
positive- and negative-frequency modes at late times. The result is thermal
particle creation at the Hawking temperature, modified in realistic cases by
the probability that each mode escapes the curved exterior.

This fixes remarkable physical facts: the black hole radiates, its temperature
is inversely related to its mass, and it loses energy. But the approximation
does not provide a unitary map from each black-hole microstate to a distinct
outgoing radiation history. In the leading calculation, the radiation is
controlled by coarse geometry rather than by a specified private diary.

The identifiability lesson is equally limited and equally important. Spectrum,
temperature, flux, and response are exterior observables. They do not identify
the hidden state-to-record channel. The project's blind-pump and coded-radiation
controls are concrete demonstrations of this logical underdetermination.

### Page: what a typical pure state looks like

Page asked a mathematical question about a random pure state divided into two
subsystems. When one subsystem is much smaller, it is almost maximally entangled
with the other. Applied to evaporation, the radiation entropy initially grows
as the radiation Hilbert space grows; after the radiation becomes the larger
side, its entropy falls if the total state remains pure.

The Page curve is therefore a powerful destination and typical-entanglement
result. It is not an emission law. The tensor split, dimensions, purity, and
typical-state distribution are inputs to the argument. No particular diary is
followed through a Hamiltonian, and no observer's record is specified. A Page
curve can be correct while the access time of a small protected diary varies
from early to final emission.

### Hayden--Preskill: recovery from a supplied encoder

Hayden and Preskill sharpened the question by throwing a diary into an old
black hole. If the old hole is already highly entangled with early radiation
and its subsequent dynamics act as a sufficiently rapid random encoder, then
an observer holding the early radiation can reconstruct the diary after
collecting only a little additional radiation, delayed by scrambling.

This is a recovery theorem, not merely an entropy curve. It establishes a
decoder under explicit assumptions. Those assumptions carry much of the
physics: the factorization into black hole and radiation, the randomness or
design quality of the dynamics, the observer's access to early radiation, and
the coupling that emits new degrees of freedom. The theorem tells a microscopic
model what it must realize; it does not show that every evaporating black hole
realizes it.

## Islands and replica wormholes

### What islands actually establish

The original proposal understated the island result by describing it mainly as
destination and entropy bookkeeping. In the controlled evaporating models,
the quantum extremal surface changes near the Page time. After that transition,
an interior region -- the island -- belongs to the entanglement wedge of the
radiation. Entanglement-wedge reconstruction then says that operators in that
region have representatives acting on the radiation code subspace.

That is algebraic access. It is stronger than saying only that the radiation
entropy decreases. Penington's analysis also derives a Hayden--Preskill-style
decoding criterion: information thrown in after the Page time becomes encoded
in the radiation after the appropriate delay, with the reconstructible code
space depending on time and on what is known about the initial state. The
Almheiri--Engelhardt--Marolf--Maxfield analysis similarly finds that infalling
information leaves the black hole's entanglement wedge after a scrambling
time in its model.

The island rule by itself stops short of a microscopic ledger of emissions. It
identifies which algebra can reconstruct an interior operator without deriving
a time-ordered set of BFSS D0 transitions, Hawking wavepackets, or multitime
correlators whose state dependence can be inspected record by record. Other
effective models supply that additional layer, as described below.

There are also qualifications. Reconstruction is code-subspace and sometimes
state dependent. It can require knowledge of the initial state or a restricted
family of states. Existence need not imply computational feasibility. These are
limits on the kind of recovery established, not reasons to demote it to mere
entropy bookkeeping.

### Why replica wormholes deserve their own row

Replica wormholes are the gravitational path-integral mechanism used to derive
the island saddle. In calculating moments such as `Tr rho_R^n`, new saddles
connect the replicas. Their competition produces the Page transition and,
after analytic continuation, the island formula for entropy.

This is related to but not identical with the statement that the island lies in
the radiation wedge. One row concerns how gravity computes the entropy; the
other concerns what the resulting wedge implies about operator reconstruction.

The factorization puzzle must also remain a question rather than a slogan.
Replica-connected geometries can resemble ensemble averages, and ensemble
interpretations are exact in some low-dimensional models. It does not follow
without further argument that every gravitational path integral simply
averages over a family of microscopic evaporation completions. In a proposed
single boundary theory, one must explain how factorization and the
replica-connected semiclassical answer coexist. Our completion language is a
useful way to state the alternatives, but it has not solved that problem.

## Effective bridges between dynamics and reconstruction

The first version of this map incorrectly treated the dynamical/reconstruction
bridge as generally absent. Several controlled models already construct it.

Liu and Vardhan's operator-gas model derives Page behavior and Hayden--Preskill
information transfer from chaotic Heisenberg evolution. Operators dynamically
develop “voids” in the remaining black hole and appear in the radiation. The
authors propose this process as a microscopic counterpart of the island
prescription. The relation to islands is a conjectured interpretation rather
than a QES derivation inside the same model, but the work supplies an explicit
dynamical information-transfer mechanism.

The Penington--Shenker--Stanford--Yang replica model goes further on recovery.
Alongside the replica-wormhole Page transition, it gives a direct gravitational
argument for entanglement-wedge reconstruction using the Petz map. This joins
the gravitational saddle to an explicit recovery map in a simple model, while
retaining the ensemble and factorization questions of the replica construction.

The most direct counterexample is Gyongyosi, Hollowood, Kumar, Legramandi, and
Talwar's *The Holographic Map of an Evaporating Black Hole*. It constructs a
temporal sequence of radiation subsets and maps a semiclassical evaporating
black hole to a microscopic model built from nested energy-block random
unitaries. The model includes energy conservation and thermal Hawking weights.
Within the same construction, the authors derive QES entropies and prove a
version of entanglement-wedge reconstruction using trace norms and quantum
fidelities. Operations on Hawking partners lying in an island can be represented
on the radiation, and infalling systems are analyzed as well.

Vardhan, Kudler-Flam, Shapourian, and Liu provide another quantitative bridge
using the equilibrium approximation. They calculate finite-temperature
radiation correlations and Petz-map recovery fidelity for an evaporating black
hole, finding a recovery onset for a large diary before the ordinary Page time.

Fixed-Hamiltonian many-body models narrow the frontier further. Chen, Qi, and
Zhang couple an SYK system to Majorana chains and calculate a Page transition
in the second Renyi entropy. A control bit inserted into the SYK system becomes
correlated with the bath after the transition, which they interpret as the
bath entanglement wedge acquiring an island. Liu and Vardhan's equilibrium
framework also explains how replica-wormhole formulas can arise for a fixed
Hamiltonian without an ensemble average. Hamiltonian studies of
Hayden--Preskill recovery in SYK and chaotic spin chains show that recovery is a
more selective property than spectral chaos alone.

These are genuine model-level answers to the question this program had called
open. They include both random-unitary and fixed-Hamiltonian examples. A
narrower top-down frontier remains: deriving the radiation sector, constrained
observable algebra, and emission coupling from a fundamental gravitational
theory while retaining enough control to demonstrate state-dependent recovery.
Large-N limits, disorder averages, equilibrium approximations, and supplied
system-bath splits still mediate the existing gravitational interpretations.
That frontier belongs to a possible successor project.

## The strict memory-burden prototype: a row changed by this program

The N-portrait and memory-burden literature proposes an entropy-sized sector
of assisted memory modes coupled to a highly occupied master mode. The full
corpuscular family contains several possible interactions and release routes.
The result here is deliberately narrower: it evaluates the canonical strict
prototype Hamiltonian used for black-hole metamorphosis and stabilization.

In that Hamiltonian, the entropy-sized degeneracy does not appear as
entropy-many independent sources at the Hawking-scale emission line. The
flux-carrying source Gram matrix has one nonzero direction. The luminosity is
enhanced by the large occupation of that collective master mode, giving
`N_eff=1` rather than `N_eff~S`.

The same Hamiltonian conserves total memory occupation. Number-number burden
couplings shift energies but do not convert a private memory pattern into
master quanta. A diary encoded inside a fixed burden sector therefore remains
absent from the emitted master record. Coarse mass or frequency tags may be
visible, while arbitrary quantum recovery is blocked in the strict model.

Two resolved strict-prototype quantities make the branch concrete. Its sharp
master source has `g2(0)=1-1/S`, and the specified transition has
emission/absorption ratio `1-1/S`. At a Hawking-scale gap, the corresponding
thermal KMS ratio is order-one different. Calling these quantities physical
black-hole radiation signatures would require an additional map from the
prototype oscillator to resolved asymptotic radiation. Rescattering, pair
annihilation, exchange terms, dressing, a continuum of modes, or additional
channels can change the result and must be treated as new completions.

## Matrix theory: mechanism on one side, channel on the other

BFSS is attractive because it does not begin with an abstract random encoder.
The black zero-brane is represented by a bound matrix state, and evaporation
can be described as an eigenvalue or D0-brane escaping the bound clump. Existing
work obtains the qualitative emission mechanism, near-thermal behavior,
negative specific heat, and accelerating evaporation.

That is genuine microscopic progress. It changes the question from “could some
unitary emit?” to “what does this Hamiltonian make the escaping sector depend
on?”

The 2024 BFSS Page-curve paper answers a neighboring question. It constructs an
effective black-hole-plus-radiation Hilbert space with time-dependent emission
probabilities and obtains the expected turnover and final purification. The
paper also states the limitation that matters here: the complete BFSS black-hole
state and the action of the full Hamiltonian on it are not known in the form
needed to derive the whole process. The factorization and emission structure
are effective inputs rather than the output of a state-resolved Hamiltonian
calculation.

The useful BFSS target is therefore not “get a Page curve.” That has been done
in an effective BFSS description. Nor is it merely “show D0 emission.” That
mechanism is already in the literature. The open BFSS derivation is:

```text
initial gauge-invariant black-hole code states
             |
             v
BFSS Hamiltonian and D0 detachment amplitudes
             |
             v
multitime algebra of escaped D0/radiation observables
             |
             v
state dependence, decoupling, and possible recovery.
```

A full calculation would constitute a new Matrix-theory program. A sharply
limited matrix element or low-order correlation could still advance the field
if it demonstrated payload dependence, payload blindness, or a symmetry
obstruction in a declared sector, but it is not required to complete this
control-model program.

## Gravitational dressing: what the two-arm proposal became

The proposed dressed pump separated two cases. In the header arm, diary choices
carry different total energy or charge. In the payload arm, they are orthogonal
private states with exactly the same declared charges. The intended question
was whether gravitational constraints reveal only the header or also the
payload.

The literature already contains the perturbative core of that comparison.
Gauge-invariant localized operators require gravitational dressing that reaches
infinity. Consequently, total Poincare charges are visible asymptotically.
But gravitational-splitting constructions show, to leading perturbative order,
subspaces whose exterior measurements are insensitive to the localized state
beyond those total charges. In the proposal's language, gravity forces a
header; it does not force a recovery-grade fixed-charge payload at that order.

There is a serious competing position. Holography-of-information arguments say
that in gravity, information on a Cauchy slice is available from an arbitrarily
small neighborhood of the boundary under suitable assumptions. If that
principle applies to the relevant exact algebra, then fixed-charge privacy can
fail beyond what perturbative splitting sees.

A generic toy pump cannot adjudicate that disagreement. If its algebra is
chosen to expose only charges, it will reproduce the header result by design.
If it is chosen to contain the full state, it will reproduce the stronger
boundary result by design. The physics lies in deriving the correct observable
algebra and dressing from a named gravitational model.

The two-arm pump is therefore retired as a standalone simulation target. It
survives as a specification test for a real model:

```text
1. identify the exact or perturbative asymptotic algebra;
2. construct different-charge and same-charge code states;
3. calculate their distinguishability in that algebra;
4. add real emission dynamics before making a claim about accumulated
   radiation records or recovery.
```

The result of this pass is already useful: header-grade metadata is a known
perturbative consequence; payload-grade access is the nonperturbative issue.

## Clocks, algebra type, and why timing is not yet access

Local quantum field algebras are type III: they do not behave like ordinary
finite tensor factors with density matrices and traces. Semiclassical gravity
changes the observable problem because diffeomorphism constraints require
observables to be defined relationally -- relative to an observer, a boundary
charge, modular flow, or some physical clock.

Witten and subsequent work showed that adjoining the appropriate flow can
produce a crossed-product type-II algebra. Type II algebras possess a trace of
the kind needed to express generalized entropy, usually up to an additive
constant. Related constructions obtain type-II algebras for de Sitter observers
and for large-N black-hole sectors.

This makes the clock connection more than an analogy. A recent analysis of
slow-roll cosmology and evaporating Schwarzschild--de Sitter black holes shows
that out-of-equilibrium dynamics can itself serve as the physical clock needed
to define gauge-invariant observables and a type-II algebra. No external clock
or asymptotic charge is required in those examples. Significantly, the resulting
algebras are not always presented as manifest crossed products.

But this does not establish the stronger conjecture in the older program note:
that the type-III-to-type-II transition is the formal shadow of “no per-record
accounting until timing metadata is adjoined.” A trace lets one define entropy
on an observable algebra. A clock lets one construct relational observables.
Neither operation, by itself, identifies a sequence of emitted records, proves
that two diary states give different histories, or supplies a recovery map.

The surviving bridge is narrower:

> Out-of-equilibrium clock dynamics can be part of the construction of the
> gravitational observable algebra on which entropy and access questions are
> well posed.

The discarded bridge is:

> Passing from type III to type II is itself the onset of diary access in the
> radiation record.

No theorem found in this pass supports the second statement, and the physical-
clock examples show that the route to type II is more general than adjoining
an external timing register.

## What is now established, and what is not

The map changes the program in four material ways.

First, the field's central gravitational result has now been placed correctly.
Islands establish entropy turnover *and* algebraic reconstruction in controlled
models. Effective random-unitary, replica, operator-gas, and equilibrium models
also connect reconstruction to explicit state-dependent radiation dynamics.
The general model-level bridge is therefore established in the literature.

Second, one proposed project calculation is no longer the right next bet. The
static header/payload distinction is substantially answered at perturbative
level, while its nonperturbative version cannot be settled by a freely designed
pump. Work should resume only inside a named gravitational algebra.

Third, two previously parallel themes do touch, but weakly. Physical clock
dynamics and type-II algebras help define what an observer can call an
observable and assign entropy to. They do not yet derive temporal information
export.

Fourth, the strict memory-burden prototype moves one row from expectation to
calculation. Entropy-sized degeneracy, active source participation, and rapid
diary recovery are independent in that Hamiltonian. The result is scoped to
the strict model, leaving dressed and interacting completions open.

The main quantum-gravity successor question is:

> Can a top-down microscopic gravity model derive both its physical radiation
> observables and a restricted gauge-invariant, state-dependent multitime
> channel, and show how they realize or revise the established reconstruction
> picture?

BFSS is one candidate because a detachment mechanism and underlying Hamiltonian
exist. It may be a poor first setting if both the radiation algebra and the
state-dependent process remain inaccessible. Existing SYK/bath and
fixed-Hamiltonian results already provide the shorter many-body bridge. Any
successor calculation should therefore add a specifically gravitational
ingredient -- a derived gauge-invariant radiation sector, dressing, a top-down
emission coupling, or a controlled failure of the effective picture -- rather
than repeat Page behavior or recovery in another generic chaotic model.

For the program that produced this map, the comparison supplies an endpoint.
The original control-model question asked whether Hawking-like thermodynamics
and unitary information return can coexist in an ordinary quantum process, and
which ingredients make that possible. The project constructions and the
literature answer yes. State count, shrinking capacity, a physical emission
channel, suitable mixing, and consistent unitary bookkeeping do distinct jobs;
thermality alone does not choose an information route. These become four model
obligations in the endpoint summary: thermodynamics, observable structure,
dynamics, and consistency. A fundamental theory must explain why a real black
hole supplies them. Pursuing that explanation would begin a new,
theory-specific research program.
