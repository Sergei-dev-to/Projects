## 1. Bottom Line

The program does **not** presently support a unique or invariant boundary
between "what gravity does" and "what quantum information does." In that
strict sense, the demarcation claim is too strong.

The work does support a narrower program:

```text
For a proposed microscopic evaporation model, determine exactly what must be
established before exterior information recovery follows; distinguish
necessary conditions, sufficient conditions, model-side calculations, and
what exterior observations can identify.
```

This is best described as an **assumption audit and conditional-closure
program for exterior recovery**. It is not a complete solution of the
black-hole information problem, and it does not need to be one.

The recommended change is substantive rather than cosmetic. The program
should stop assigning every pre-QI obligation to "gravity," stop claiming a
canonical count of gravitational inputs, and organize its claims by logical
role.

## 2. What Was Being Called Demarcation

The current five-row plan asks which ingredients gravity must supply before
ordinary statistical mechanics and QI yield thermality, Page behavior,
decoupling, and recovery:

```text
state count and softness;
physical radiation algebra and emission instrument;
temporal information-export dynamics;
geometry/interior completion.
```

As an internal dependency map, this has been productive. It prevented the
program from treating supplied finite Hilbert spaces, supplied mixers, or
supplied tensor factors as microscopic explanations. It also exposed the
failure of the attempted static source-rank certificate.

As a claim about a gravity/QI boundary, however, it fails three tests.

### 2.1 Exclusivity

A state space, observable algebra, interaction instrument, and mixing dynamics
are obligations of every microscopic open-system model. The physical emission
vertex and channel-relative mixing are not intrinsically gravitational.

Some realizations are specifically gravitational:

```text
S=A/4G and the Schwarzschild equation of state;
gravitational constraints and dressing;
the exact-to-semiclassical algebra transition;
bulk causal and interior interpretation.
```

But it is inaccurate to label all model-side dynamics "gravity" merely because
the target system is a black hole.

### 2.2 Invariance

Dual descriptions can move a phenomenon between geometric gravity and
ordinary many-body dynamics. The invariant distinction is not
"gravity versus QI," but:

```text
facts fixed or proved by the concrete microscopic theory;
versus
consequences that follow from abstract statistical/QI structure once those
facts are supplied.
```

### 2.3 Canonical decomposition

The proposed inputs can be regrouped. One may absorb the algebra into the
channel, combine the state count with thermodynamic emission, or treat the
entire evaporation history as one process tensor. Counterexamples establish
independence relative to a chosen formalization; they do not prove that nature
contains exactly three fundamental inputs.

Therefore the program may identify independent **obligations**, but should not
claim a unique inventory of gravity.

## 3. What Survives the Reassessment

Three technical lines survive without demarcation rhetoric.

### 3.1 Composable temporal-access obstruction

For a sequential, changing-shell emitter with shared memory and constrained
reachable states, closeness at each step to a diary-blind comparison process
composes linearly. If the cumulative defect remains small, no exterior decoder
can recover the diary with order-one accuracy.

This is a necessary access theorem. Its content is not the familiar slogan
that information must enter the radiation. Its candidate technical value is
the explicit composable process-distance formulation for a memoryful
evaporation history and its exact blind controls.

The corresponding sufficient statement remains conditional: process
ETH/design or equivalent radiation-resolved moment control yields decoupling
after the record-entropy threshold.

### 3.2 Static identifiability boundary

The closed source-rank branch establishes a specific family of failures:

```text
source-list representation noninvariance;
aggregate Gaussian hidden-tail nonidentifiability;
signed HIGH/LOW cancellation;
active Hawking-like static mimics;
finite single-history precision limits.
```

The broad lesson that thermal marginals do not determine information flow is
standard. The possible contribution is the assembled no-go map against a
specific proposed microscopic participation certificate and the exact
conditions under which narrower passive diagnostics survive.

### 3.3 Model-evaluation interface

The program replaces generic chaos, inclusive rates, and arbitrary source
decompositions with the physical radiation process. For Matrix detachment the
candidate interface is

```text
D_m(t) = Q_m exp(-i H t) P_E,

K_ij^(mn)(t) = sum_f A_(i->f,m)(t) A*_(j->f,n)(t),
```

with the accessible algebra, exact sectors, and daughter trace specified
first. This is a protocol for asking whether a microscopic model supplies the
conditions used by the recovery theorem. It is an application interface, not
itself a result about BFSS black holes.

## 4. Revised Organizing Question

Replace

```text
What inputs does gravity contribute before QI takes over?
```

with

```text
What must a microscopic evaporation model demonstrate before unitary
evaporation and exterior recoverability follow, which conclusions then follow
universally, and which obligations can exterior data identify?
```

This produces four model obligations without asserting that they are a unique
ontology:

1. **Thermodynamic obligation:** specify the state count and physical
   energy/charge dependence relevant to emission.
2. **Subsystem obligation:** define the accessible radiation algebra, its
   centers/sectors, and the complementary hidden or daughter algebra.
3. **Dynamical obligation:** show that the actual time-ordered emission process
   becomes sufficiently diary-visible and isotropically mixing before the
   available evaporation time ends.
4. **Consistency obligation:** show composability, shrinking capacity,
   conservation laws, and the disposition of partners or hidden archives.

Once these are supplied, standard statistical mechanics and QI may imply
thermal ratios, entropy thresholds, decoupling, and recovery. Which obligations
have a gravitational interpretation is then a model-specific question.

## 5. Three Levels That Must Not Be Conflated

```text
ontological/model condition:
  what the microscopic evaporation process actually satisfies;

computational certificate:
  what can be calculated in a Hamiltonian, path integral, lattice model, or
  simulator;

operational certificate:
  what an exterior observer can infer from feasible radiation interventions.
```

Much of the static branch failed because it tried to infer a model-side source
structure from an operationally compressed record. Conversely, a BFSS
transition-tensor calculation could verify a model condition without being an
astrophysically feasible experiment. Future claims must label the level.

## 6. Publication Consequences

The work should not be forced into one flagship "demarcation theorem."

```text
paper_access_latency_classification:
  technical necessary-access theorem, sufficient comparison, and exact
  controls;

paper_boundary_saturation:
  conditional passive theorem plus the source-certificate no-go boundary;

synthesis/perspective:
  assumption audit for exterior evaporation claims, including the division
  between model obligations and universal consequences;

Matrix/BFSS:
  optional model application, only if a gauge-invariant physical calculation
  clears the feasibility gates.
```

The synthesis should explicitly acknowledge that conditional Page/decoupling,
the broad thermality-versus-information distinction, quantum-channel language,
and the algebra-type seam are occupied territory. Its value would be the
precise dependency and identifiability map, not priority over those ideas.

## 7. Program Survival Test

The reframed umbrella deserves to survive only if at least one of the following
is established after a full theorem and literature audit:

```text
1. The composable access obstruction is materially sharper than a routine
   restatement of standard hybrid/channel continuity bounds in this setting.

2. The assembled static no-go family excludes a live class of black-hole
   inferences more sharply than generic inverse-problem folklore.

3. The model-evaluation interface reduces a real microscopic calculation to a
   smaller, independently computable set of observables rather than requiring
   full process tomography or direct recovery as an assumption.

4. The combined framework changes an actual conclusion about a named
   microscopic evaporation model.
```

If none survives, retire the umbrella and publish only any standalone technical
lemmas that remain worthwhile. Do not preserve a program merely because it has
accumulated machinery.

## 8. Recommendation

```text
Strict gravity/QI demarcation:
  RETIRE as the primary claim.

Five-row map:
  RETAIN as an internal coverage map, not a unique division of nature.

Primary scientific program:
  REFRAME as necessary/sufficient/identifiable obligations for exterior
  information recovery in microscopic evaporation models.

Immediate work:
  consolidate and independently audit the access theorem and static no-go
  package before investing in the Matrix pilot.

Matrix pilot:
  KEEP as a gated application, subordinate to the theorem audit.
```

The strongest defensible summary is:

> The program audits what a microscopic evaporation model has and has not
> established when it claims unitary evaporation with exterior information
> recovery. It proves
> process-level access obstructions, identifies limits of static radiation
> inference, and supplies a model-facing test interface. It does not claim a
> unique metaphysical boundary between gravity and quantum information.
