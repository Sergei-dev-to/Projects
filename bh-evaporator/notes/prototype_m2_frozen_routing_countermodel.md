# M2 Frozen-Routing Countermodel Sketch

Date: 2026-07-05

Status: theorem sketch plus verified countermodel.  The strict
memory-burden statements are checked against the TeX sources through
`prototype_m0_m1_results.md`; the general converse remains a target,
not a proved theorem.

## Purpose

M2 should be recorded as more than a latency estimate.  The strict
Dvali memory-burden prototype is a physical realization of the
frozen-routing arm: records keep flowing through the master radiation
mode, while memory-sector diary information has no Hamiltonian route
into that record.

This gives the negative/countermodel half of the access-latency story.
It does not yet prove the converse: under which assumptions HP-fast
models must have either rapid mixing into the coupling algebra or
nonlocal/constraint-dressed emission access.

## Strict Prototype Claim

Consider diaries encoded in a fixed diagonal burden sector of the
memory modes, so that coarse total energy/frequency tags do not already
identify the diary.  In the strict prototype:

```text
source Gram at the flux line:       N_eff = 1
memory occupation:                  N_m conserved
memory-to-master ladder routing:    absent
master-to-radiation record:         present
fixed-sector diary recovery:        impossible at all times
```

The emitted master record may depend on coarse diagonal burden data
through frequency or mass shifts.  That can leak classical tags in
non-equal-gap variants, and it matches the mass-spread hair emphasized
in Dvali:2024hsb.  It is not Hayden-Preskill recovery of an arbitrary
new quantum diary.

## Proof Skeleton

Let `A_D` be the algebra of diary perturbations supported on the memory
sector, and let `A_emit` be the algebra generated by the master emission
channel and its emitted records.

The strict Hamiltonian has three structural facts:

```text
1. the flux-carrying exchange component is {a0, b0};
2. master-memory coupling is number-number, so it dresses a0 by
   functions of memory occupations rather than rotating a0 into memory
   ladder operators;
3. total memory occupation N_m is conserved, and memory hopping stays
   inside the memory sector.
```

Therefore the Heisenberg evolution of the coherence-carrying part of a
fixed-sector diary operator has zero ladder overlap with the master
emission algebra:

```text
P_emit[ U(t) O_D U(t)^\dagger ] = 0
```

for the part of `O_D` carrying the diary coherence.  The record channel
can at most condition on diagonal burden data.  For fixed diagonal tags,
all emitted records are independent of the diary coherence, so the
optimal recovery fidelity remains at the no-information baseline.

The latency is therefore infinite in the strict toy for fixed-sector
quantum diaries.  In the black-hole mapping, the available release
route is the rare paired memory annihilation channel of Dvali:2024hsb.
That paper gives a model-dependent post-burden law
`tau ~ R S_BH^(1+k)` with `k > 0`, and the pair-annihilation argument
gives the conservative bound `tau >= R S_BH^2`.  This is
parametrically incompatible with HP `R log S` latency.

## General Theorem Target

Define a routing-overlap profile for a diary algebra `A_D` and a source
coupling algebra `A_src`:

```text
G_D(t) = sup_{O in A_D, ||O||=1} || P_src[ U(t) O U(t)^\dagger ] ||
```

where `P_src` is projection onto the operator subspace visible to the
emission map.  In a factorized compressed channel whose emitted records
are generated only from `A_src`, HP recovery of arbitrary new deposits
should require `G_D(t)` to become order one by the recovery time, unless
the emission map is already nonlocal/constraint-dressed on `A_D`.

The memory-burden prototype has `G_D(t)=0` for fixed-sector diary
coherences in the strict model.  It is the countermodel arm of the
alternatives theorem:

```text
fast recovery requires routing/mixing into the coupling algebra
or nonlocal emission access;
frozen routing gives record flow without private recovery.
```

## Drafting Discipline

- Do not state that all memory deposits are invisible.  Deposits that
  change diagonal energy/load data may be visible as coarse classical
  tags.
- Do not call the radiation statistics "Dicke-like" unless a concrete
  visibility or `g2` calculation is attached.
- Do not claim that the positive boundary-ETH branch derives HP
  latency from source rank alone.  It needs the usual scrambling and
  decoupling hypotheses in addition to `N_eff ~ S`.
- Do cite the Dvali corpus' own collective-mode equivalence: equal
  coupling to `K` memory modes reduces to one collective mode, with the
  other `K-1` modes decoupled.
