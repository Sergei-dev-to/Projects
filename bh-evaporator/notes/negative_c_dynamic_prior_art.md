# Prior Art: Negative Heat Capacity Dynamically Coupled to Page-Like Information Flow

## Question

Has someone already built a model that dynamically combines:

```text
negative heat capacity / convex microcanonical entropy
explicit quantum evaporation dynamics
Page-like radiation entropy
non-gravitational finite system or collision model
```

## Short answer

I did not find this exact combination.

There is substantial adjacent literature, but the apparent gap remains:

```text
Page-like dynamics without gravity exists.
Negative heat capacity / black-hole thermodynamics exists.
Open-system thermodynamic Page curves exist.
Island/Page curves for actual negative-C black holes exist.

But an explicit non-gravitational microscopic evaporator whose emission dynamics
are driven by a negative microcanonical heat-capacity schedule appears distinct.
```

## Closest adjacent work

### Glatthard 2024/2025

Glatthard shows Page-curve-like entropy dynamics in open quantum systems and
then studies the thermodynamics of that entropy decrease in Markovian systems.

Relevance:

```text
very close on Page-like open-system thermodynamics
```

Difference:

```text
ordinary relaxing/cooling systems
not a negative-C_mu evaporator that heats up while losing energy
not an accelerating black-hole-like emission law
```

### Ray-Dhar-Kulkarni / quantum trajectory Page dynamics

Recent interacting-system and reservoir papers show Page-like entanglement
dynamics in spin chains, fermionic systems, and trajectory settings.

Relevance:

```text
Page-like dynamics in many-body/open systems
```

Difference:

```text
not built around negative heat capacity or convex intruder thermodynamics
```

### Hotta and Sugita 2015

Reference:

```text
Masahiro Hotta and Ayumu Sugita,
"The fall of the black hole firewall: Natural nonmaximal entanglement
for the Page curve",
Progress of Theoretical and Experimental Physics 2015, 123B04.
```

Important point:

```text
canonical typicality gives nonmaximal entanglement for general systems;
canonical heat capacity is nonnegative;
black holes have negative heat capacity;
therefore black-hole negative heat capacity modifies the usual Page-typicality
reasoning.
```

Relevance:

```text
directly connects negative black-hole heat capacity to Page-curve reasoning
```

Difference:

```text
does not construct a non-gravitational finite-system evaporator
does not use convex-intruder microcanonical thermodynamics as a lab engine
does not simulate outgoing time-bin radiation with unitary collision dynamics
```

### Island/Page-curve papers for specific black holes

Many gravitational papers compute Page curves for black holes with different
parameters, charges, accelerations, or modified gravity theories. Some mention
negative heat capacity as part of the black-hole thermodynamic background.

Relevance:

```text
negative-C black-hole thermodynamics + Page curves
```

Difference:

```text
gravity/island mechanism, not a non-gravitational quantum evaporator
```

### Quantum thermodynamics of negative heat capacity

There is literature on negative heat capacity in finite systems, long-range
systems, microcanonical thermodynamics, strong coupling, and quantum
thermodynamics.

Relevance:

```text
supports the finite-system thermodynamic engine
```

Difference:

```text
not usually tied to unitary Page-like radiation entropy
```

## Search conclusion

The gap is narrower than:

```text
Page curve outside gravity
```

which is already well covered.

The plausible niche is:

```text
explicit non-gravitational unitary evaporation dynamics
whose emission schedule is generated by negative microcanonical heat capacity
and whose radiation entropy is computed from emitted quantum time bins.
```

## Consequence for Step 2

Step 2 should be designed so the negative-C schedule is not cosmetic.

It must make these quantities come from the same model:

```text
shell degeneracies D(E)
microcanonical beta(E)
emission probability / energy loss
radiation entropy
Page-like turnover
```

If the collision model is merely:

```text
an excited system relaxing into a cold bath
```

then it is too close to Glatthard.

If it is:

```text
a convex-intruder core that heats up as it emits and whose emission accelerates
while radiation entropy follows a computed Page-like curve
```

then it appears meaningfully distinct.
