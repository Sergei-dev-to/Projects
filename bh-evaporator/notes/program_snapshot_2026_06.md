# Program snapshot: what we learned, what opened (2026-06-12)

Condensed state of the demarcation program after the trilogy converged
(long paper + operational-horizon note + companion-in-progress) and the
dS reservoir lemma. Written before the dS positioning read, so the
"opened" items are pre-literature hypotheses.

## What we learned

1. **The model has no slack — the inputs are the phenomenology.**
   Necessity trinity: state count ⟺ temperature law, boundary channels
   ⟺ luminosity law, decoupling ⟺ complementary recovery. Nothing in
   the construction is a discretionary modeling choice.

2. **The horizon splits into operational and geometric halves, and the
   operational half is not gravitational.** Thermality, area-law
   absorption, Page/island, recovery switching, state-dependent
   mirrors, complexity barrier: all follow from the three inputs in
   factorized QM. Geometric residue: local structure only (Rindler
   correlators, QNM spectrum, infalling experience).

3. **Coarse thermodynamics underdetermines fine structure; the Page
   time is the meter.** The orthogonal-μ variant matches every coarse
   observable yet is fine-grained-distinguishable from Schwarzschild;
   the Page time measures the per-quantum record capacity ν. Unitarity
   forces the collective jump (the field carries no emitter label);
   shared outgoing modes cap record capacity and would mediate any
   emission-induced scrambling.

4. **The scrambling–temperature tie is genuinely gravitational.** The
   factored model can scramble arbitrarily fast (a microcanonical shell
   is infinite-temperature for the mixer alone). Gravity gets the MSS
   bound at β only because energy dynamics and scrambling dynamics are
   one Hamiltonian. That unity is an import, not a consequence.

5. **All of Schwarzschild's non-locality lives in the spectrum.** The
   entropy gap is 't Hooft/Susskind/Bousso lore; ours is the
   factorization: the rate condition is locally achievable, so the
   state count carries the entire holographic burden. Under
   energy-sharing, the inputs force Dvali–Gomez corpuscular kinematics;
   the residue is the stabilizing interaction, not the microstructure.

6. **dS is the mirror image** (reservoir lemma): the dS state count is
   an ordinary finite bath through second order (C_eff = S_0).
   Schwarzschild = exotic spectrum, pedestrian constraints; de Sitter =
   pedestrian spectrum, gravitational content forced into the
   constraint structure and the channel saturation.

## What opened

**A. Boundary saturation as the invariant — candidate new central
question.** Exactly one input is nontrivial for *both* horizons:
N ~ S — all entropy sits in the coupling-accessible contact cells
(ordinary 3d bath: N ~ S^{2/3}). The spectrum input flips from
impossible (Schwarzschild) to trivial (dS); saturation survives both.
Hypothesis: the operational definition of a horizon, full stop, is *a
thermal system whose entire entropy is coupling-accessible*, and "why
does gravity put all the entropy where the coupling is" is the single
question under both papers. Only became visible by running the same
demarcation on both horizons; the positioning read cannot have
preempted it in this form — check anyway. If it holds, it may deserve
treatment before Stage B.

**B. The e^{-S_0} layer as one subject.** Half-wormhole corrections,
constraint-induced correlations, and holography-of-information
availability all live at O(e^{-S_0}). Stage B may literally reduce to
"exact vs. e^{-S_0}". Direction 2's variance technology is the shared
instrument; three projects may be one question about the exponentially
small layer.

**C. The two-stage constraint foil, design-justified.** Since dS input
1 is generic, Stage B is where the dS answer has to be (modulo A).
See ds_operational_horizon.md.

**D. The stabilization target, sharpened.** From "find a
microstructure" to "find the critical interaction for forced soft
large-N kinematics"; day-one theorem: negative heat capacity ⇒
ensemble inequivalence ⇒ non-additivity ⇒ long-range attraction
(Lynden-Bell/Thirring/Campa–Dauxois–Ruffo lore + corpuscular target).
Companion-paper material, parallel to everything else.

## Converging residue list

Across all demarcations run so far, the genuinely gravitational
residues are: (i) why the spectrum (Schwarzschild only); (ii) why
boundary saturation N ~ S (both horizons — see A); (iii) the
constraint/dressing structure (dS, holography of information); (iv)
local geometry. Everything else examined to date has landed on the
quantum-mechanics side.
