## 1. Compact Conditional Theorem: OCCUPIED

The statement

```text
given a finite black-hole system/radiation split, unitary rapid mixing, and an
appropriate shrinking channel, Page/decoupling/Hayden-Preskill behavior is
ordinary quantum information;
```

is standard.  Hayden--Preskill explicitly assumes unitary rapid mixing and
control of Hawking radiation, then derives rapid recovery from random-subsystem
decoupling [arXiv:0708.4025](https://arxiv.org/abs/0708.4025).  Harlow's
Jerusalem lectures package the information-paradox/QI framework and its
assumptions [arXiv:1409.1231](https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.1231).  The modern
island review explains how fine-grained Hawking-radiation entropy is computed
gravitationally [arXiv:2006.06872](https://arxiv.org/abs/2006.06872).

**Scoop verdict:** do not sell the compact conditional theorem by itself as
new.  It is the organizing baseline against which the program localizes the
remaining gravitational inputs.

## 2. Thermal Marginals Do Not Determine Information Flow: OCCUPIED BROADLY

The broad claim that locally thermal Hawking modes can coexist with nontrivial
global information/correlation structure is explicit in continuous-variable
form in Aurell--Eckstein--Horodecki
[arXiv:2012.14418](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.14418).  Mathur's small-corrections
theorem establishes a different but adjacent limitation: small corrections to
the Hawking pair state do not restore unitary evaporation
[arXiv:0909.1038](https://arxiv.org/abs/0909.1038).  The island/replica-wormhole
literature likewise makes clear that the fine-grained entropy problem is not
settled by the leading thermal spectrum
[arXiv:1911.12333](https://arxiv.org/abs/1911.12333).

**Scoop verdict:** “the information paradox could not be settled by exterior
flux thermality” is not ours as a broad insight.

**Unoccupied technical residue found in this pass:** the exact adversarial
package aimed at *microscopic source participation*--source/exterior
refactorization, arbitrary Gaussian Gram-tail decomposition, signed response
and fourth-moment cancellation, active greybody mimic, and single-history
precision budget--was not found assembled as one certificate no-go.
