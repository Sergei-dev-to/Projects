## 1. Program Question

Which inputs must gravity or a microscopic gravity model supply before
ordinary quantum mechanics, statistical mechanics, and quantum information
explain black-hole evaporation?

The current answer is:

```text
gravity/microscopic theory supplies:
  state count and softness;
  the physical radiation algebra and active emission instrument;
  a temporal process that actually mixes private information into radiation;
  the geometric/interior interpretation;

ordinary QI/stat mech supplies, conditionally:
  density-of-states thermality;
  Page behavior;
  decoupling and recovery;
  complementary reconstruction on a supplied code/algebra.
```

The program's distinctive result is not this broad conditional slogan. It is
the theorem-backed localization of what exterior Hawking data cannot supply
and of the invariant dynamical condition that replaces static microscopic
source rank.

## 2. Authoritative Five-Row Map

Question:

```text

S=A/4G and Schwarzschild S(E)~E^2?
```

Status:

```text
open for generic Schwarzschild;
realized or supported in special string, matrix, and holographic regimes;
not natural in ordinary finite-density local matter without additional
large-N, fractionated, long-string, edge, or constraint structure.
```

Demarcation:

```text
This is a gravitational/holographic input. QI consumes the dimension but does
not derive it.
```



```text

notation supplied by QI.
```

### Row 4: Temporal encoding and information export

```text
Does the physical time-ordered emission process become diary-visible and mix
all private directions sufficiently for decoupling before evaporation ends?
```

```text
conditional theorem stack closed;
```


Hayden--Preskill behavior alone as new.

```text

signed HIGH/LOW cancellation;
the active Hawking-like Gaussian mimic;
```

Microscopic source participation may still be defined inside a canonical model,
but it is supplied model structure, not a generally observable black-hole
input. Do not reopen static tomography, extra moments, drain settings, or a QNM
specialization unless a tombstone reopening condition is met.

### Long-time necessary access theorem

Complete. Hybrid telescoping and CPTP contractivity give a linear cumulative
process-defect bound for shared-memory, energy-constrained, changing-shell
emitters. The finite active pump is the exact zero-access control.

### Kinematic blind/mixing controls

Complete. Shrinking-shell, no-hiding, locally identical coded-radiation, and
integrated microcanonical collision constructions distinguish:

```text
energy export;
record/history rank;
cumulative diary access;
decoupling and recovery.
```

Further abstract emitters are not on the critical path.

## 6. Work Selection Rules

Every new task must answer at least one:

```text
Does it derive or constrain state count/softness?
Does it define the physical radiation algebra in a gravity model?
Does it test the actual emission process for diary access/decoupling?
Does it clarify the exact-to-semiclassical algebra transition?
Does it address geometric/interior completion?
Does it consolidate a publishable theorem package already obtained?
```

Reject or defer work that merely:

```text
adds another toy channel with supplied mixing;
adds static moments to infer hidden microscopic source lists;
tests generic chaos without the physical emission operator;
computes inclusive rates without process moments;
renames standard Page/decoupling consequences;
opens Kerr/JT/dS variants before the core package is stable.
```

## 7. Current Stop Conditions

The Matrix route pauses if:

```text
no gauge-invariant detachment/radiation algebra can be defined at the chosen
truncation;
the calculation only produces classical trajectories or inclusive rates;
truncation/gauge errors exceed the process-moment signal;
the accessible radiation labels cannot be separated from hidden daughter or
partner labels;
the required Hilbert space is beyond local resources and no collaboration/data
path is identified.
```

If paused, the program does not return to generic toys. It consolidates the
demarcation theorem package and records microscopic verification as the open
gravitational problem.
