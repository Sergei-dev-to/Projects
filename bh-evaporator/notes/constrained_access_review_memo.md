## Thesis

The useful object is the private complement of a public classical
interface.

Redundant records can make a commuting sector label public:

```tex
\mathcal A_{\rm code}
=
\bigoplus_x \mathcal B(\mathcal H_x),
\qquad
\mathcal Z(\mathcal A_{\rm code})
=
\operatorname{span}\{P_x\}.
```

The noncommuting block degrees inside fixed `x` are private relative to
those records.  The question is not whether the public center exists;
that is largely decoherence / Quantum Darwinism / no-broadcasting.  The
question is what happens to the private complement:

```text
erased or hidden,
slowly recoverable by transport or sampling,
quickly recoverable by scrambling/routing,
or visible through nonlocal/dressed access.
```

The horizon-like case is a high-structure constrained-access interface:
public no-hair data, private microscopic blocks, source-side saturated
access, and fast private recovery only after the relevant access
enlargement or dressing.

## Current Result Stack

The formal source is `paper_access_latency_classification/main.tex`.

**Cut theorem.**  Exact redundant recoverability from two disjoint record
fragments forces the recoverable algebra to be commutative.  A nontrivial
matrix block would be broadcast by the two recovery maps, contradicting
no-broadcasting.

Consequence:

```text
redundant public access -> classical center;
noncommuting block degrees -> private to those fragments.
```

**Reduced-record latency theorem.**  In a bounded-degree finite system
with finite-range dynamics and source-local emission near an access
region `X`, a diary deposited at distance `L` from `X` is absent from the
reduced emitted record before the Lieb-Robinson routing time:

```tex
\|\mathcal N_t-\mathcal C_t\|_\diamond
\le
C|X|\,|Y|\,t e^{-\mu(L-vt)} .
```

Therefore no decoder on that record, even computationally unbounded, can
recover the diary with high entanglement fidelity in that window.

**Saturation/latency separation.**  Source-side saturation,
`N_eff(W)=S`, does not imply fast private recovery.  A channel can have
`S` participating source cells while each record samples only one cell;
a diary in cell `j` is sampled with probability at most `n/S` after `n`
records, so order-one recovery advantage requires `n=Omega(S)`.

**Anonymous saturation does not force mixing.**  A finite-dimensional
collective-charge pointer model has:

```text
S source qubits,
J_i = sigma_i^z,
N_eff(W)=S,
Q = sum_i sigma_i^z,
U = sum_q P_q \otimes V^q.
```

It is unitary, record-label anonymous, permutation symmetric, and
source-saturated.  It redundantly records only the collective charge
sector.  The exponentially large noncommuting blocks inside fixed charge
sectors remain private forever.  Thus

```text
saturation + anonymity + source symmetry + unitarity
does not imply mixing or private recovery.
```

**Horizon-interface separation.**  For a family indexed by size `S`,
assume:

```text
redundant passive records recover a sector label;
nontrivial private blocks exist inside sectors;
source-side access is saturated, N_eff(W)=Theta(S);
arbitrary k-qubit private deposits are recoverable from the allowed
enlarged record/access algebra in sub-power-law depth.
```

Then ordinary finite-velocity source-local emission from a geometrically
local source with worst-case diary-to-access distance `L(S)=S^alpha`
cannot realize the profile.  Such an interface needs at least one of:

```text
fast internal routing/scrambling;
nonlocal/dressed access.
```

**Frozen-dynamics diagnostic.**  Freeze the internal source dynamics
while keeping the record coupling near the access region.  If private
recovery disappears, the protocol used fast routing/scrambling.  If
private recovery survives, the recovering algebra was not generated by
source-local emission in the factorized variables, or the side
information already contained the diary.  This makes the fast-routing
versus dressed-access fork operational.

**Protected-complement commutant theorem.**  For the record-generating
interaction algebra `A_rec`, finite-dimensional noiseless-subsystem
structure gives

```tex
\mathcal H=\bigoplus_\alpha
\mathcal H_\alpha^L\otimes\mathcal H_\alpha^R,
\qquad
\mathcal A_{\rm rec}
=
\bigoplus_\alpha
\mathcal B(\mathcal H_\alpha^L)\otimes I_\alpha^R,
```

and

```tex
\mathcal A_{\rm rec}'
=
\bigoplus_\alpha
I_\alpha^L\otimes\mathcal B(\mathcal H_\alpha^R).
```

The exact private complement protected from passive records is the
noncommuting part of the commutant.  Mixing inside a public block is
structurally forced only when `A_rec` acts irreducibly there, leaving
only scalars in the commutant.

## What Is Known Versus New

Known or imported:

- Redundant objective records select commuting classical information
  through no-broadcasting, Quantum Darwinism, and spectrum broadcast
  structure.
- Private/correctable complement ideas overlap with private subsystems,
  complementary channels, information-disturbance, and operator-algebra
  QEC.
- Collective-sector private blocks resemble decoherence-free/noiseless
  subsystem examples.
- HP recovery, Harlow-Hayden/Python's lunch, and dressed/Gauss-law
  access are standard black-hole-information ingredients.

Program-specific composition:

- Treat the private complement of a public classical interface as the
  central object.
- Classify private-complement recovery by access latency and access
  algebra.
- Separate source-side saturation from recoverability.
- Show that anonymous symmetric unitary saturated monitoring can still
  stop at a public center.
- Identify the fast horizon-like branches as fast routing/scrambling or
  nonlocal/dressed access.

## Remaining Sharp Questions

1. Which approximate objectivity theorem gives the cleanest quantitative
   version of the cut theorem?

2. Is the source-local reduced-record theorem stated with the right
   channel model and citations for the environment/field coupling?

3. Is `N_eff(W)` the right invariant for source-side saturation after
   physically normalized coupling strengths are fixed?

4. Can the fast-routing and nonlocal/dressed branches be separated by an
   operational diagnostic rather than by description?  The current
   candidate is the frozen-dynamics diagnostic.

5. What additional condition, if any, upgrades anonymous saturated access
   into genuine mixing rather than collective-charge monitoring?  The
   structural language should be the interaction algebra and its
   commutant/noiseless subsystems; that structural result is now in the
  draft.  The new question is the quantitative rate at which a generic
  or ETH-like record-generating algebra becomes effectively irreducible
  inside each public block.  The draft packages that as an effective
  irreducibility rate `lambda`, giving protected-component collapse
  after `n >= lambda^{-1} log(C/epsilon)` records once `lambda` is
  established for a model.

6. Does the horizon specialization require predicates beyond public
   center, private blocks, compression/anonymity, source saturation,
   Page/HP or dressed recovery, latency, and complexity discipline?
