# Wormhole Paper Reference Checklist

This is a seed checklist, not a finished bibliography.

## High-priority concepts to search for
- charge without charge
- wormhole electric flux
- asymptotic charge in wormholes
- Gauss law on nontrivial topology
- harmonic forms in wormhole geometries
- cohomology and electromagnetic charge
- field lines through wormhole throat
- traversable wormhole charged particle transport
- monopole versus multipole wormhole signatures

## Authors / names likely worth checking
- John Wheeler
- Misner and Wheeler
- Matt Visser
- Sergey Krasnikov
- Dai and Stojkovic
- Sushkov
- Morris and Thorne
- authors discussing observational wormhole electromagnetism or lensing signatures

## Questions for literature scan
1. Who states or implies that moving charge through a wormhole mouth can create an asymptotic monopole at an end?
2. Are there papers that clearly distinguish topological flux sectors from local charge transport?
3. Is there an existing theorem or explicit electrostatic calculation that already proves the intended rigidity statement?
4. Which papers are worth engaging directly, with quotations?
5. Are there known Green functions or image-charge constructions for simple wormhole electrostatics?

## What to collect for each candidate paper
- full citation
- exact claim relevant to monopole rigidity
- whether the claim is explicit, implicit, or merely suggestive
- whether it is wrong, ambiguous, or consistent with the target thesis
- whether it is worth citing in the main text or only in notes

## Likely literature roles
- Historical background:
  - Misner-Wheeler / Wheeler “charge without charge”
  - classical wormhole texts or reviews
- Technical background:
  - electrostatics on wormhole backgrounds
  - Gauss law / topology / cohomology treatments
- Targets for correction or clarification:
  - papers suggesting monopole creation by ordinary transport
  - phenomenology papers using that intuition

  - role: standard review/book background
  - status: added to `references.bib`

### Electrostatics / charge prior art
- Nail R. Khusnutdinov and Ilya V. Bakhmatov, `Self-force of a point charge in the space-time of a symmetric wormhole` (2007)
  - role: electrostatics/self-force prior art
  - status: added to `references.bib`
  - question: do they already separate monopole sector from source motion?
- Sergei Krasnikov, `Electrostatic interaction of a pointlike charge with a wormhole` (2008)
  - role: likely closest technical prior art
  - status: added to `references.bib`
  - question: does Krasnikov already state the fixed-sector/no-varying-monopole point in nearly final form?
- Eduardo Guendelman et al., `Hiding Charge in a Wormhole` (2011)
  - role: useful for charge/flux-sector language
  - status: added to `references.bib`

### Main target chain
- De-Chang Dai and Dejan Stojkovic, `Observing a wormhole` (2019)
  - role: main target paper
  - status: added to `references.bib`

- Sergei Krasnikov, `Comment on "Observing a wormhole"` (2020)
  - role: existing rebuttal/comment
  - status: added to `references.bib`

- De-Chang Dai and Dejan Stojkovic, `Reply to "Comment on 'Observing a wormhole'"` (2020)
  - role: clarifies how DS defend the claim after pushback
  - status: added to `references.bib`
