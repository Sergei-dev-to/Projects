# Wormhole Project Board

## Priority
Primary current gravity project.

## One-sentence claim
Asymptotic monopole charges at the ends of a two-ended wormhole are topological sector data and cannot be changed by ordinary transport of charge through the throat.

## Why this project now
- strongest current gravity-paper candidate
- cleaner and less crowded than several black-hole projects
- likely arXiv-worthy with moderate effort
- easiest to judge for completion

## Expected effort
Medium.

## Progress so far
Conceptual framing is strong, but no consolidated draft has been surfaced yet from project files.

## Main risks
- literature may already contain the exact statement in essentially final form
- explicit field calculation may be harder to package cleanly than expected
- literature targets for correction may be weaker or fuzzier than hoped

## Kill conditions
- the exact rigidity theorem already exists in a standard reference and the remaining paper would add only exposition
- no explicit literature claim worth correcting can be identified
- explicit example turns out not to clarify more than the abstract theorem

## Best-case outcome
A solid technical clarification paper with:
- one precise proposition
- one vivid explicit example or figure
- one restrained literature diagnosis

## Immediate next tasks
1. scan project folders for relevant assets
2. identify existing calculations / notebooks / notes
3. formulate the proposition precisely
4. choose the easiest explicit model/example
5. assemble initial bibliography
